## The model: decisions already made with Joseph

These were talked through and agreed, not assumed.

- **A causal loop diagram, not a bow-tie.** The bow-tie (source → hazard → threat → event → effect → consequence, with controls and escalation factors) is a *view*: the acyclic slice around one event. Joseph's reason: *"we can basically create a weighted causal loop out of the elements to some degree or another — or at least one or two level dag."*
- **Nodes are quantities** ("strength of safety culture"), so edges can carry signs.
- **Good and bad live on edges, not nodes.** Joseph: *"It allows things to be both good and bad at the same time, as is common in causal loop modeled systems."* "Control", "escalation factor" and "escalation control" are roles an edge gives a node. They are not node types.
- **Edge weight:** signed, roughly logarithmic ±1/±2/±3, plus `±?` and `?`. **Evidence status is kept separate:** intuition < mechanism < precursor < incident < established. Joseph was comfortable with this. He is instinctively headed toward system dynamics, hence the `sd_kind`/`units` hooks.
- **Explicit open ends.** `unknown-causes` and `unknown-events` placeholder nodes. His three cases:
  - a capability or change that feels bad for reasons not yet known;
  - a known effect to prevent with unknown causes (his example: *devaluing other human life*);
  - an observed unprecedented change with no named event yet (his example: *problem-solving dependency*).
- **The lexicon lives on the node** (`aisi_term`, `aliases` verbatim with location, `not_same_as`). The terminology map and the causal model are one artifact.
- **AISI's vocabulary is the default where it exists.** AISI now has a real glossary: *Loss of Oversight* (May 2026, relata `aisi-2026-loss-oversight`, App. C). Its operative terms for sub-catastrophic events are "unsanctioned behaviour/action", "event" and "incident".

**The design agreed with Joseph (four layers):**
- **Terms:** `model/terms/def-*.ud`, in udon, in the house style of `~/src/arch/firmatum/udon/v2/references/def/*.ud`. The things and kinds (lab, model, commitment …); these need not be quantities. Each term group carries a per-term `|lexical-map` table (Joseph's idea): each source's word with its match (exact / close / broad / narrow / related / homonym), a verbatim quote and its location.
- **Quantities:** the existing `concepts*.yaml`. These are the only nodes causal claims connect. Joseph: something that causes by existing is written as a quantity of it ("existence of X", "degree of capability of X"). Each quantity will gain `of:` naming its term(s).
- **Causal claims:** `relations*.yaml`. The structural relations now in there (`part_of`, `indicates`) move to the term layer.
- **Lexicon:** `mappings*.yaml` for quantity-level mappings. Term-level mappings go in the term files.

Format: udon for terms now (no parser yet; `check.py` will need only term ids); YAML for the rest until a udon parser exists.
