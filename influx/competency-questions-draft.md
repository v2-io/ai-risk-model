# Competency questions: a first draft, for Joseph

*Candidates only, drafted 2026-10-06 by Claude (Opus 5.5) for decision 12 in `MODEL-GAMMA-METHODOLOGY-AND-PLAN.md`. They are first-pass and need Joseph's rewriting, cutting or replacing. The method is described in the plan's §3.11: questions written before building, which the finished model must be able to answer, and which decide what machinery earns a place.*

The aim in `CLAUDE.md` is a map that can be projected "by cause, by control, by risk event and impact, and by who is affected and who decides". Each question below names the axis it serves and the parts of the plan it would exercise. Questions 1, 3 and 4 are the de novo review's examples; the rest are mine.

| # | Question | Axis | What it exercises |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For a single incident with 60 deaths, which sources would call it catastrophic, severe or systemic, under what conditions, and through how many independent lineages? | impact | severity bars as structured qualifiers; resolution of "catastrophic / severe / systemic"; lineage-aware counting |
| 2 | For a given focal event (say, a model copying its own weights outside its developer's control), which sources treat it as a possible event, which as a cause of some other event, and which report it as having happened? | event; cause | stage as a role relative to a focal event; events vs scenarios; factuality per source |
| 3 | For loss of control, which preventive controls do sources name, and which of them are duties with no holder of the correlative claim? | control | the norm layering (Hohfeld correlatives); controls as records; resolution of "loss of control" |
| 4 | Which actors write into which agent part, and which sources' role words resolve to each? | cause; who decides | actors and agent parts; institutional vs functional roles; translations |
| 5 | When a frontier model uses deception against its developer outside an evaluation, who must do what, by when, under which instruments, and with what consequence if they don't? | who decides | norms (deontic type, trigger, deadline, sanction); legal status over time; SB 53, the EU Code and company frameworks side by side |
| 6 | Where do two sources use the same word for things that differ enough to change what a reader would conclude, and which reading does each intend? | (all) | collisions; resolution records with outcome and reason; declared ambiguity |
| 7 | How many independent lines of evidence support a given claim (for example, that capability thresholds trigger safeguards), once copies, shared authors and restatements are counted once? | (all) | lineage relations; claim identity as an attributed assertion; the corroboration view |
| 8 | Which risks does any source say cannot yet be measured or evaluated, and what does each source say should be done in the meantime? | event; control | evidence-state assertions; technical norms vs prescriptions; precautionary postures |
| 9 | Which harms fall on people or shared goods that are not users or customers, which sources name them, and who does each source hold accountable? | who is affected | impact targets (systems and shared goods); owed-alignment referents; accountability roles |
| 10 | What did a company framework change between versions, did the company say so, and did the change strengthen or weaken a commitment? | who decides | document versions; lineage subtypes (revision, announcement status, drift direction); norm strength |
| 11 | Which decisions does each source reserve, and to whom: who may decide to deploy, pause, or override a review body? | who decides | Hohfeld powers and immunities; decision-authority assertions |

**Not yet covered, worth deciding whether they should be:** questions about the alignment referent itself (waiting on the alignment-referents spike), about recovery and response after an event (the NRR's territory), and about the project's own claims, kept separate from the sources'.
