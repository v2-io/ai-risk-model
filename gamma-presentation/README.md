# gamma-presentation/

A guided tour of `MODEL-GAMMA-METHODOLOGY-AND-PLAN.md`, built for Joseph on 2026-10-08 by Claude (Opus 5.5) as a Design canvas, "Model gamma — a guided tour". It is meant to take about an hour on its main line, against an estimated four for the plan itself.

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `questions.md` | Sixteen questions to the plan's author, raised while building the tour, with the author's answers inline (2026-10-08) |
| `canvas/` | A copy of the canvas's source: `canvas.json` (the index) and one `.dc.html` per artboard. The live canvas is the artifact; this copy is for reference and may drift from it |

The tour reorganizes the plan around decisions. Five parts: orientation (why gamma; what the sources are), the machinery (architecture, lexicon, translation and resolution, assertions, passages and lineage), the domain (risk, hazard and instances, actors), the proof so far and the plan (the G1b thin pass, where a definition's precision lives, competency questions, phases), and a decision board with 24 decisions (the plan's 13, plus lettered ones from the thin pass, §6 and the questions). An addendum lists every point of the plan the tour doesn't carry, with the reason.

Same model family as the plan, its research and its reviews: where the tour agrees with them, that is coherence, not confirmation.
