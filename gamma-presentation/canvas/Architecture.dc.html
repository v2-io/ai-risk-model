Almost every layer of the model already has an established vocabulary. What none of them has is your address theory’s resolution: ambiguity as an outcome, as of a moment.

Three research agents read the established literatures on 6 October (influx/gamma-research/). Their main result: most of model beta’s coinages already have names, and adopting the names brings better theory along with them.

Two directions of work

concept ⟶ our name for it

Onomasiological: the lexicon

Settle the concept, then choose its name. ISO 860: “Harmonization starts at the concept level and continues at the term level”; in ISO 704, assigning a designation comes last. This is how “hazard” and “developer” should be decided (chapters 9 and 10).

a source’s word, in its scope ⟶ which concept of ours

Semasiological: the translations

Start from the word as used and ask what it denotes. Temmerman (2000) argues that real special languages need both directions, and that polysemy and drift in them are normal, not defects.

We don’t harmonize the sources with each other. ISO 860 says harmonization works where a field “is well established and relatively stable” and has “a tradition of standardization”. Frontier-AI risk is neither, which gives compilation-not-adjudication a citable reason: map each source onto ours, and leave them unreconciled with each other.

Evidence note: the ISO standards were read in their official previews, and Temmerman via a secondary source; the citation check found the plan drops that second mark.

Strategy from Evans, mechanism from you

Two vocabularies describe the method itself, and the plan wants the seam between them explicit in def/.

Domain-driven design supplies the strategy. A bounded context is “a description of a boundary … within which a particular model is defined and applicable”. A context map says “Map the existing terrain. Take up transformations later.” The relationship patterns name what sources do to each other: xAI adopting the Code’s terms is conformist (RAISE copying SB 53 word for word counts only under a widening the def/ entries propose, since copying alone doesn’t show an upstream-downstream relation); an anticorruption layer is “an isolating layer … in terms of your own domain model”, which is what a translation is.

Address theory supplies the mechanism: reference and referent with intended cardinality; binding against match; scope with containment; resolution from an origin, as of a moment, under declared admissibility and preference; ambiguity as an outcome; dangles and collisions. You know this part better than anyone; skim it.

The join: Evans’s bounded contexts are flat, the sources’ scopes nest, so a bounded context is defined as a kind of scope. Address theory is imported by reference at commit 90f4fb6 of v2-io/udon, and the pin moves only deliberately, with the affected entries re-read.

Question E3Answered by the plan’s author, 8 Oct

Two DDD patterns were rejected for cases they seemed to fit (the RSP’s “plain meaning” is deliberately unbound, not separate ways; a self-inconsistent source is several scopes, not a big ball of mud). Is DDD becoming naming only?

No. DDD still does the strategy and the diagnosis: each source as its own bounded context, the context map, the anticorruption layer that keeps our model from conforming, the conformist diagnosis of beta, and the ubiquitous-language discipline. Address theory does the structure. Two patterns failing two cases isn’t a rejection of the strategy. def/README.md will say this; queued.

Optional depth

What exists already

def/ holds six ddd: term groups, twenty terms, all proposed, drafted 7 October. Evans’s definitions are verbatim for eight terms; his patterns are restated with ISO’s [SOURCE: …, modified — …] marks.

The entries add blocks your address-theory entries don’t have: |conditions, |practices, |collisions, |proposed-widening and |ambiguity, so that what a term is stays apart from when it arises and what its author advises.

ddd:bounded-context declares an ambiguity in Evans himself: the definition says “a description of a boundary”, the pattern text uses it as the region.

Why the address-theory import isn’t a shared kernel

Nothing is held jointly in this repository, and no second team’s consultation gates a change. Grok’s audit caught the first draft calling it one. The nearest pattern is conformist: adopted untranslated.
