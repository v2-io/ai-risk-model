Question A2Answered by the plan’s author

Is a source namespace inside def/ still an anticorruption layer? Yes, if the boundary is drawn this way. Evans’s layer is about whose model governs ours, not where the files live. Beta adopted IASR’s definitions as our terms; hosting them verbatim in an iasr26: namespace is a different act. Each source namespace is a foreign bounded context we keep a copy of; arm: alone is our ubiquitous language; the layer is the relations between them. An arm: term never takes a source definition as its own, and an arm: umbrella contains the arm: concept a source term maps exactly to, never the source term. ddd: is the one exception: Evans’s terms adopted verbatim as our method vocabulary, the narrow cited adoption you have described. def/README.md should name the two kinds of namespace, ours and hosted, once Decision A is made.

Your sketch line, re-expressed

arm:thousands-severe-harm (== pf:severe-harm, != (fgf:severe-harm == arm:50-severe-harm))

The material keeps its shape and corrects two equalities. The PF’s severe harm contains two classes, one of them a dollar class, so it isn’t equal to a casualty class. The FGF’s says “including”, so the 50-death class is a member of an open class, not the whole. The != survives as :not-to-be-confused-with between two senses, and which sense the FGF’s word carries is a resolution of each use, not a relation between terms.

Your worry about open and closed

“Grammatical pedantry”? In a definition, no: “means” against “includes” is the source’s choice between a floor and an example, and in the thin pass’s 40-death variant it changed results. Counting rules (pooling, what “a single incident” governs) turned up inside SB 53’s definition too. In claims, probably yes: leave the default unmarked and mark only where an answer changes.

The trial’s rules for arm:

A term exists only where two sources meet or our thinking needs one. No judgment words in names (“catastrophic”, “severe”, “systemic” appear only in source namespaces); this did the most work. Our umbrellas contain only our terms.

Strains, from notes.md §2–3

Ambiguity in four places: a referent, an attribute value, a relation (:{exact | narrower}), a whole set. One mark held for all four, but braced relation keys are new syntax.
Brackets collide in udon: braces already mean reference and cardinality, and def-resolution.ud already uses | for a type union. Options: udon list brackets for sets, or keywords one-of(), all-of(), any-of().
? does two jobs; . does two, and facets needed a third form (=).
Descriptive names grow with every characteristic. Better: harm classes carry facets, and the name is a handle.
Can’t yet say: who asserts a relation (:by); time (@version, :in-force); inheritance (:inherits with overrides); evaluation, which needs an interpreter.
