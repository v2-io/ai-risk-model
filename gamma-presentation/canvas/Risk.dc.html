“Risk” can’t be one formal object. Following the Society for Risk Analysis, it becomes a declared umbrella over named readings, built from components the field already agrees on.

The SRA Glossary gives seven qualitative definitions of risk and a separate list of metrics, and says of the metrics: “None of these examples can be viewed as risk itself”. The plan proposes saying in def/ that we follow that separation.

The components are close to canonical

Kaplan and Garrick’s triplets, ISO 31000, the SRA’s risk description, NIST SP 800-30, the UN’s disaster-risk terms, bow-tie and Reason’s barriers decompose risk into nearly the same parts:

a sourcea cause or initiating conditiona focal eventcontrols, preventive and mitigativewhat degrades the controlsconsequence or harmmagnitudelikelihoodexposure, vulnerability, capacitythe knowledge the judgment rests on

The last is the one most often missed: the SRA’s (C′, Q, K) puts background knowledge and its strength inside the risk description, and the UK’s PHIA pairs a probability with a separate analytical-confidence rating.

It is also acyclic around its one event, and the cycles you expect live between events (one event’s consequence is another’s cause), so a bow-tie can show at most a local slice. “Stage is a role relative to a focal event” has its precedent here.

Already in the corpus: IASR 2026 defines the “bowtie method” in its table of risk-management practices, citing Koessler and Schuett. The plan says Buhl et al. “recommend” it; the citation check found they say it “may be useful”.

Why “risk” is an umbrella, not one object

Kaplan and Garrick would make risk the set of triplets ⟨scenario, probability, consequence⟩, so a probability, an expected value and a single scored scenario are all projections of it. Several senses in the corpus are not projections of that set:

STAMP: “the effectiveness of the controls”, a property of a control structure (STPA Handbook p. 133). AISI’s Loss of Oversight “severity” reads this way.
ISO 31000: “effect of uncertainty on objectives”, relative to someone’s objectives, upside included.
IPCC: counts risks that arise from the responses themselves.
MIT: the unit is a risk description, a structured statement some source wrote.
Hansson’s fifth sense: a decision under known probabilities, a property of the epistemic situation.

Optional depth

What the components must carry

Knowledge apart from likelihood (SRA, PHIA, NRR).

Likelihood’s proposition, window and conditioning. PHIA probabilities apply to propositions, past ones included, so the EU Code’s “likelihood that a causal link exists” is the same instrument on a different proposition.

An explicit “not yet thought of” scenario. Kaplan and Garrick treat it as a first-class scenario assessed on evidence: a precedent for your “unknown” risk events.

Whether a reading admits upside (ISO, NIST). The harm baseline: counterfactual in NIST 600-1 and Anthropic, absent in Guide 51.

Impact targets that are systems or shared goods; recoverability as its own axis, which is where the loss-of-control definitions split; tolerability: ALARP and “gross disproportion”.

A lead, not a lineage claim

The HSE’s Reducing Risks, Protecting People (2001) proposes treating as intolerable a risk of an accident killing 50 or more in a single event at more than one in five thousand a year. SB 53’s bar is more than 50 people from a single incident. Unchecked; the citation check also found the plan’s R2P2 quotation is not verbatim.

Decision 4“Risk” as a declared umbrella with named readings

Illustrative names: risk as a possible event, a source, a likelihood, an expectation, a scenario set, deviation from objectives, control inadequacy; and a risk description. A translation then says which reading a source uses, or “ambiguous among …”.

Lean: high on the structure, open on the names
Decision 11The impact radius

Target (a group of people, or a system or shared good) × degree × recoverability. Exposure, vulnerability and capacity go under target; the NRR’s banded scales are one source vocabulary for degree.

Lean: adopt, moderate; “degree” least worked out
