What could make the plan wrong, even where it is accurate

The plan’s §6, each risk with its guard.

The borrowed vocabularies are heavier than the work needs

Guard: take their distinctions and names, not their machinery; an entry that imports machinery without a corpus case is the failure. The thin pass used none of chapter 6’s machinery; Decision G holds it.

Same-model coherence

The plan, the three research reports, the spike, the thin pass, both de novo passes, the citation check and this tour are all one model family. Agreement among them is coherence, not confirmation. Guard: the primaries they quote, your review, and readers from other families.

Lexicon first can still harden too early

Guard: the thin passes, which show which terms bear weight before most are refined, and G4, whose pilots are expected to change the lexicon before any wide translation.

First-pass entries can look finished

Fluent drafts read as settled and get inherited, as beta’s term files were. Guard: your refinement passes, the supported status, G4’s disagreement data.

Over-weighting the strict sources

The round trip favours statutes. The loose sources test whether declared ambiguity stays honest instead of becoming a dumping ground, which the MIT test doesn’t catch. Guard: the IASR round trip and its rule for when an ambiguity passes.

Conflict of interest

Anthropic’s August Risk Report is a pilot and the main example of a source with its own vocabulary, judged by an Anthropic model. Guard: the EU Code as a pilot beside it, and two-translator coding across model families.

Decision KA cross-family review, now, before G4Lean: high

Grok 4.7’s audit of the plan and the first def/ drafts (7 October) was the first reader from another family, and its accepted changes are in; §6 doesn’t yet record it (my question D1; queued). Still without one: the alignment-referents spike; the thin pass and its notation; the research reports’ quotations; the citation check; the def/ revisions made after Grok’s audit. G4’s two-translator coding assumes translators from different families, and one review would test that cheaply first.
