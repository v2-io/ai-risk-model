# Unit 14 · L102–104 · p.7: Measure 1.1 end: (4) updating process; the deadline

**What it held.**
- (4) "a description of the process by which Signatories will update the Framework, including how they will determine that an updated Framework is **confirmed**, as specified in Measure 1.3."
- "Signatories will have confirmed the Framework no later than **four weeks after having notified the Commission pursuant to Article 52(1) AI Act** and no later than **two weeks before placing the model on the market**."

**What differed.**
- I predicted the deadline's shape (N weeks after the Art. 52 notification; before market). The numbers are four weeks and two weeks, and **both** apply: whichever comes first, effectively. I didn't remember the numbers.
- The deadline is for the Framework to be **confirmed**, not created or written. "Confirmed" is a term of art here, with its own process ("how they will determine that an updated Framework is confirmed"). I didn't predict it. It implies an internal approval step: someone with authority signs off. Probably defined later (Glossary or Commitment 8: management body?).
- The list in M1.1 has exactly four items; no item on serious incidents or external evaluators in the Framework itself.

**Wandering.**

- **"Confirmed"** carries the governance weight. A Framework that's written but not confirmed doesn't count. Who confirms? My guess: the management body, or a body Commitment 8 names. If the Code had said "approved by the board", that would be strong; "confirmed" with a self-described process is flexible. For our lexicon: "confirmed (Framework)" is a state with its own criteria, set by the signatory.
- **The two clocks.** Art. 52(1) AI Act: a provider notifies the Commission within two weeks once its model meets (or it knows it will meet) the systemic-risk threshold (10^25 FLOP presumption), as I recall. So the sequence: threshold met (or foreseen) → notify within 2 weeks → Framework confirmed within 4 weeks after that → and at least 2 weeks before market. If a model crosses the threshold during training and release is far off, the 4-week clock governs. If release is imminent, the 2-weeks-before-market clock governs. Either way, the Framework exists before the model is placed on the market.
- **But note: "placing the model on the market".** The singular "the model" in a Measure about a firm-level Framework. Read literally: the model the Art. 52 notification is about. For a firm with an existing Framework, this deadline is moot after the first model; updates fall under M1.3. A slight drafting wrinkle: the deadline is written as if every signatory starts from zero with one model.
- **Internal use isn't on either clock.** The deadline is keyed to notification and to *placing on the market*. A model used only internally, never placed on the market, triggers the Art. 52 notification (if it meets the threshold, the notification duty arises when it's met, I believe, regardless of market) but not the market clock. So for internal deployment the 4-week clock is what applies. That's consistent, at least. But it confirms my unit-12 worry: the Code's coverage of "using" leans on the Art. 52 trigger.
- **Pace.** The Measure is fully specified now: high-level description of processes; trigger points (justified); acceptance criteria and tiers (justified); mitigations per tier; timelines to exceed the highest tier (justified, probabilistic allowed); whether outsiders influence go/no-go; responsibility allocation; update process including confirmation. A compact if-then-governance template. The SB 53 expert will see a "frontier AI framework" with overlapping elements; I'd expect the overlap to be substantial because both draw on the same lab-framework lineage. Not something to assert from here.

**Questions to keep.**
- "When must the Framework exist?" M1.1: confirmed "no later than four weeks after having notified the Commission pursuant to Article 52(1) AI Act and no later than two weeks before placing the model on the market."
- "What does 'confirmed' mean for a Framework?" Self-described process (M1.1(4), M1.3); look for who confirms.
- Should be asked but might not be: "Does a model only used internally trigger the Framework deadline?" Via Art. 52(1) notification, not via the market clock.

**Prediction for unit 15.** Measure 1.2, Implementing the Framework. I expect: Signatories will implement the processes in the Framework; and paragraph 2 lists (1)(a) the lighter-touch evaluations at trigger points (already cross-referenced), (1)(b) "continuous" monitoring post-market (serious incidents, user reports), and (2) full systemic risk assessments before placing a model on the market ("as specified in Commitment 2/3"). Possibly "Signatories will implement the Framework's processes for each model" and something about the Model Report.
