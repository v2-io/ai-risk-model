# Unit 108 · L864–870 · p.38: Appendix 3.1, Rigorous model evaluations

**What it held.** "Signatories will ensure that the model evaluations are conducted with high scientific and technical rigour, ensuring: (1) internal validity; (2) external validity; and (3) reproducibility."

**What differed.** As predicted. The three Glossary properties become the operative requirement. All the "may be shown by, e.g." material lives in the Glossary. So for a fork: the obligation is here (3.1); the guidance on *how to show* it is in the Glossary entries.

**Wandering.** This is a place where the Code's layout scatters meaning: Appendix 3.1 is three words; the Glossary carries the substance (internal validity's long list, external validity's documentation of divergence, reproducibility's release of evaluation materials to the AI Office). An extraction that takes Appendix 3.1 alone would lose almost everything. Cross-link always.

**Questions to keep.** "What rigour do evaluations need?" Appendix 3.1: internal validity, external validity, reproducibility, as defined (with examples of demonstration) in the Glossary.

**Prediction for unit 109.** Appendix 3.2 Model elicitation: "Signatories will ensure model evaluations elicit the model's capabilities, propensities, affordances and/or effects with at least state-of-the-art techniques… minimising the risk of under-elicitation and of model deception during testing ("sandbagging")… e.g. by adapting test-time compute, rate limits, scaffolding, tools, fine-tuning, prompt engineering, best-of-n sampling… matching the capabilities of potential misuse actors… and testing with mitigations removed."
