# Unit 93 · L764–766 · p.35: Appendix 1.3, Sources of systemic risks (introduction)

**What it held.** "The following **model capabilities, model propensities, model affordances, and contextual factors** are treated as **non-exhaustive, potential** systemic risk sources for the purpose of systemic risk identification (pursuant to Commitment 2)."

**What differed.** The contributing characteristics stopped at six. I'd predicted more (novelty/lack of mitigations, covertness, monoculture, vulnerable groups): none. So the nature list is short: three essential, six contributing. "Covert" risks and correlated failure across models aren't among the named characteristics.

The four source categories here match my prediction (memory), and match M3.2's evaluation objects, with **"contextual factors"** in place of M3.2's "effects".

**Wandering.**
- **Evaluation objects vs risk sources**: M3.2 evaluates capabilities, propensities, affordances, *effects*; Appendix 1.3's sources are capabilities, propensities, affordances, *contextual factors*. Effects are outcomes (what evaluations try to measure); contextual factors are inputs (deployment conditions). The two lists are deliberately different in their fourth member. A small, telling precision: the Code distinguishes what you measure from what produces risk.
- **"Non-exhaustive, potential"**: a doubly hedged list. Not all sources; and each is only potentially a source. The signatory's process may find others.
- **Missing contributing characteristics, imagined**: "covert" (risk that materialises without detection, e.g. a deceptive model) and "correlated across providers" (monoculture: all frontier models share a vulnerability) are both systemic in a finance sense and absent from 1.2.2. Deception is in the Glossary; maybe it shows up as a propensity in 1.3. Correlation across providers is a gap worth flagging: the Code's own design encourages shared methods (recital (e), forecasts with other providers), which raises exactly this correlation.

**Questions to keep.**
- "What are the sources of systemic risk in the Code?" Appendix 1.3: model capabilities, model propensities, model affordances, contextual factors ("non-exhaustive, potential").
- Should be asked but might not be: "Does the Code treat correlated failures across models (monoculture) as a systemic characteristic?" Not among Appendix 1.2's characteristics.
