# Unit 17 · L139 · p.9: Figure 3 caption

**What it held.** "Figure 3. Full systemic risk assessment and mitigation process. The text of the Commitments and Measures takes precedence." The figure itself, on p.9, isn't in the canonical text.

**What differed.** I predicted Measure 1.3. Instead, a third figure, which apparently takes much of p.9 alone. Three figures in four pages, all about process: the Framework cycle (Fig. 1), the lifecycle timeline (Fig. 2), the full assessment loop (Fig. 3).

**Wandering.**
- Each figure carries the same precedence disclaimer. Repetition like that suggests the drafters worried readers would treat the figures as the Code, probably because the figures are simpler than the text and might show a cleaner loop than the text actually requires (e.g. omitting the "if not acceptable" condition or the "starting from point (1)" re-entry).
- For a fork: the text is fully sufficient, but if anyone asks "what does the Code's process diagram show?", the answer is that the canonical text lacks the figures, and they're on PDF pp. 6, 8, 9. Someone reconstructing them should do it from M1.2, not from memory of the figures. (I've never seen them in this reading.)
- The heaviest visual investment in the Chapter is in Commitment 1. That signals what the drafters think is hardest to grasp: the lifecycle and the loop, not the content of risks.

**Prediction for unit 18.** Measure 1.3, as predicted at unit 16: updates at least every 12 months and on material changes; confirmation of updates; perhaps a "Framework assessment" for adequacy, triggered by specified conditions.
