# Unit 77 · L615–619 · p.28: Commitment 10, Additional documentation and transparency

**What it held.** "**LEGAL TEXT:** Articles 53(1)(a) and AI Act". "Signatories commit to documenting the implementation of this Chapter (as specified in Measure 10.1) and **publish summarised versions** of their Framework and Model Reports **as necessary** (as specified in Measure 10.2)."

**Conversion note:** "Articles 53(1)(a) and AI Act" has lost an article number, like C1's LEGAL TEXT line (unit 11). Probably "Articles 53(1)(a) and 55(1) AI Act" or similar. Also "this Chapter(as specified" lacks a space. Check PDF p.28 before citing.

**What differed.** As predicted, including "as necessary". C10 cites **Art. 53(1)(a)**: the general GPAI documentation duty (technical documentation for the AI Office). So C10 links this Chapter's documentation into the Art. 53 technical documentation regime that applies to all GPAI providers.

**Wandering.**
- **The first public-transparency Commitment, and it's "as necessary".** All the Chapter's main artefacts (Framework, Model Report) go to the AI Office confidentially. C10 is the only route to the public: *summarised* versions, *as necessary*. The decisive question is what "necessary" means, which M10.2 must answer. My memory says something like "if and insofar as necessary to assess and/or mitigate systemic risks". If so, public transparency is instrumental to risk mitigation, not a value of its own.
- **Documentation of implementation** (M10.1) is likely the retention and record-keeping duty, probably 10 years (my unit-0 memory).
- The Chapter's arc: risk process (C1–C4), mitigations (C5–C6), reporting to regulator (C7), governance (C8), incidents (C9), documentation and public transparency (C10). Ten Commitments, as I remembered. Then appendices.

**Questions to keep.**
- "Must Frameworks and Model Reports be published?" C10: summarised versions "as necessary" (M10.2). Read M10.2 for the test.

**Prediction for unit 78.** Measure 10.1, Additional documentation: Signatories will draw up and keep up to date documentation relevant to their obligations under Art. 55: (1) a detailed description of the model architecture and training (perhaps), (2) evaluation strategy and results, (3) how they implemented the Chapter's Measures; kept for at least 10 years after placing the model on the market; provided to the AI Office on request. Also "keep track of non-adherence"? Maybe documentation of the Framework assessment.
