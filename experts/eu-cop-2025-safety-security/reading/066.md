# Unit 66 · L530–537 · p.24: Measure 8.2, Allocation of appropriate resources

**What it held.** "Signatories will ensure that their **management bodies oversee the allocation of resources** to those who have been assigned responsibilities (pursuant to Measure 8.1) that are appropriate for the systemic risks stemming from their models. The allocation of such resources will include: (1) human resources; (2) financial resources; (3) access to information and knowledge; and (4) computational resources."

**What differed.** Exactly my predicted four. One thing I didn't expect: the duty is for the **management bodies to oversee the allocation**, not a direct duty that resources be sufficient. The Measure governs who decides, not how much.

**Wandering.**
- **No quantity.** No "X% of compute for safety" (cf. OpenAI's 2023 superalignment pledge of 20% compute, later controversial). The Code requires appropriateness and board oversight. Resource commitments were a known flashpoint; the Code chose governance over numbers. A fork asked "does the Code require a percentage of compute for safety?" should say no; M8.2 requires board oversight of appropriate resource allocation, including computational resources.
- **"Access to information and knowledge"** as a resource: the risk function must be able to know what research and product are doing (e.g. early access to models and training plans). In practice, the risk function's biggest problem in fast labs is often not budget but information. Naming it is perceptive.
- **Computational resources** for those with risk responsibilities: evaluation compute, red-teaming compute, interpretability compute. In a compute-constrained lab, safety teams compete with capability teams for GPUs. Board oversight is the Code's protection.
- Short Measure. The governance Commitments are mostly structural; their bite depends on M7.2(3)'s disclosure of how decisions were made, and on the AI Office's scrutiny.

**Questions to keep.**
- "Does the Code require resources for safety work?" M8.2: management bodies oversee the allocation of appropriate human, financial, informational and computational resources to those with systemic-risk responsibilities. No quantities.

**Prediction for unit 67.** Measure 8.3, Promotion of a healthy risk culture: Signatories will promote a healthy risk culture, e.g. by setting the tone from the top, enabling open communication and challenge of decisions, ensuring independence and incentives of risk staff, informing staff of the Framework, providing anonymous reporting channels (whistleblowing), not retaliating against staff who raise concerns, surveying staff about risk culture. And "without prejudice to" the Whistleblower Directive (recital (d)).
