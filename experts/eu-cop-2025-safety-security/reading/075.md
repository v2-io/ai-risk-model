# Unit 75 · L605–607 · p.27: M9.3 final report; bundling similar incidents

**What it held.** "Signatories will submit a **final report**, covering all the information required by Measure 9.2…, not later than **60 days after the serious incident has been resolved**." "If multiple similar serious incidents occur within the reporting timelines, Signatories **may include them in the report(s) of the first serious incident**, while respecting the timelines for reporting for the first serious incident."

**What differed.** The 60-day final report was in my prediction (memory). Bundling of similar incidents: not predicted.

**Wandering.**
- **"Resolved"** is undefined. Who decides when an incident is resolved? The provider. An incident never declared resolved never gets a final report but stays in four-weekly updates. Fine either way for oversight.
- **Bundling** is practical (a misuse campaign generating many incidents) and slightly risky: a cluster of incidents reported as one may obscure scale. "Similar" again undefined. The first incident's timeline governs, so bundling can't delay reporting.
- **No "exceptional circumstances" explanation yet.** M9.3's opening "save in exceptional circumstances" isn't explained. Maybe never. A fork should mark it as an undefined escape valve.
- The overall incident regime is a full pipeline: tracking (M9.1), content (M9.2), initial/intermediate/final timelines (M9.3), and presumably retention (M9.4). The most procedurally complete Commitment after C1 and C7.

**Questions to keep.**
- "When is the final incident report due?" "Not later than 60 days after the serious incident has been resolved", covering all M9.2 information.
- "Can similar incidents be reported together?" Yes, within the first incident's report(s) and timelines.
- Should be asked but might not be: "What are the 'exceptional circumstances' that excuse missing a deadline?" Undefined (so far).

**Prediction for unit 76.** Measure 9.4, Retention period: Signatories will keep documentation of all relevant information under this Commitment for at least five years from the date of documentation or the date of the serious incident, whichever is later, without prejudice to Union law. Then Commitment 10.
