# Unit 76 · L609–611 · p.27: Measure 9.4, Retention period

**What it held.** "Signatories will keep documentation of all relevant information gathered in adhering to this Commitment for **at least five years** from the date of the documentation or the date of the serious incident, whichever is later, without prejudice to Union law applicable to such information."

**What differed.** Exactly predicted. My unit-0 memory said "10 years?" for documentation retention: that may be a different retention (C10, for general documentation) or simply wrong. Five years is the incident retention.

**Wandering.**
- **"Without prejudice to Union law"**: GDPR's storage limitation may require deleting personal data sooner; the Code doesn't override it. So incident records may be retained in anonymised form.
- **"Whichever is later"**: retention runs from the later of documentation or incident date. A pre-incident log (recital (j)) relevant to a later incident gets five years from the incident.
- That ends C9. The incident regime covers pre-market incidents ("entire model lifecycle"), uses Art. 3(49)-like categories plus cyber, triggers on reasonable suspicion, and has 2/5/10/15-day initial deadlines, 4-weekly updates, 60-day final report, 5-year retention.

**Questions to keep.**
- "How long must incident documentation be kept?" At least five years from the documentation or the incident, whichever is later (M9.4).

**Prediction for unit 77.** Commitment 10, Additional documentation and transparency: Signatories commit to documenting the implementation of this Chapter and keeping it for at least 10 years after the model is placed on the market (that's my "10 years?"), and to publishing summarised versions of their Framework and Model Reports "if and insofar as necessary to assess and/or mitigate systemic risks", with removal of sensitive information. Measures 10.1 (additional documentation) and 10.2 (public transparency).
