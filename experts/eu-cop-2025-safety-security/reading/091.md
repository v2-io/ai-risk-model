# Unit 91 · L749–753 · p.35: Appendix 1.2.1, Essential characteristics

**What it held.** "(1) The risk **is specific to high-impact capabilities** pursuant to Article 3(65) and Article 3(64) AI Act. (2) The risk **has significant impact on the Union market** pursuant to Article 3(65) AI Act. (3) Said impact **can be propagated at scale across the value chain** pursuant to Article 3(65) AI Act."

**What differed.** Exactly my prediction, three elements, verbatim decomposition of Art. 3(65). No added interpretation of "specific".

**Wandering.**
- **The decomposition is useful and lossy.** Art. 3(65) had "due to their reach, or due to actual or reasonably foreseeable negative effects on [the five objects]". The essential characteristics drop the "due to" clause. So "significant impact on the Union market" stands alone here, without the reach-or-effects route. The five objects reappear as Appendix 1.1's types instead. So the Code splits Art. 3(65) into: types (the objects) + essential characteristics (capability-specificity, Union impact, propagation). Reasonable, but a fork should note that "reach" as a route to significance is in the Act's definition (quoted in unit 88) and not in 1.2.1's restatement.
- **"Has" vs "can be"**: (2) "has significant impact", (3) "can be propagated". The tense asymmetry from Art. 3(65) persists. Read with "reasonably foreseeable" from the Act: "has" in the sense of a risk whose impact would be significant.
- **"Specific to high-impact capabilities"** is the hardest condition to apply. Is misinformation "specific to" high-impact capabilities? Only if frontier capability makes it materially worse. CBRN uplift: yes, the capability is the frontier's. So the essential characteristics favour capability-driven risks and filter generic ones. That's why the five types' broad examples (CSAM, privacy…) will mostly not survive as *systemic* unless frontier capability makes them distinctive.
- These are bolded in the source: the drafters emphasise the operative words.

**Questions to keep.**
- "What are the essential characteristics of a systemic risk?" Appendix 1.2.1: specific to high-impact capabilities (Art. 3(64)/(65)); significant impact on the Union market; impact can be propagated at scale across the value chain.
- Should be asked but might not be: "Where did 'due to their reach' go?" It's in the quoted Art. 3(65), not in 1.2.1's restatement.

**Prediction for unit 92.** Appendix 1.2.2 Contributing characteristics, a longer list: the risk (1) can arise from capabilities/propensities/affordances; (2) involves actors (malicious, the model itself); (3) has high probability-severity; (4) speed; (5) irreversibility; (6) cascading or recursive dynamics; (7) novelty / lack of established mitigations; (8) large scale/reach; (9) low visibility (covert); (10) affects vulnerable groups or large populations; (11) asymmetric (offence-defence) dynamics.
