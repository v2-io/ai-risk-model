# Unit 78 · L621–630 · p.28: Measure 10.1, Additional documentation (first part)

**What it held.** "Signatories will draw up and keep up-to-date the following information **for the purpose of providing it to the AI Office upon request**:
1. a **detailed** description of the model's architecture;
2. a detailed description of how the model is **integrated into AI systems**, explaining how software components build or feed into each other and integrate into the overall processing, **insofar as the Signatory is aware** of such information;
3. a detailed description of the model evaluations conducted…, including their results and strategies; and
4. a detailed description of the safety mitigations implemented…."
"Documentation will be retained **at least 10 years after the model has been placed on the market**. Further, Signatories will keep track of the following information, to the extent it is not already covered…, for the purpose of **evidencing adherence** to this Chapter to the AI Office upon request:" (continues)

**What differed.** As predicted: on-request documentation, 10-year retention (so my unit-0 "10 years" memory belonged here, not to incidents). The contrast with the Model Report: here everything is "**detailed**", where the Model Report asked for "high-level" descriptions (M7.1(1)). Two tiers: high-level in the submitted report, detailed on request.

**Wandering.**
- **Two tiers of disclosure to the regulator.** Submitted: Model Report (high-level architecture; detailed justification). On request: detailed architecture, integration, evaluations, mitigations. The AI Office gets summaries by default and depth when it asks. That's an efficient regulator design, if the AI Office has the capacity to ask well.
- **Integration into AI systems "insofar as the Signatory is aware"**: the system-level picture is required only as far as the provider knows it. For its own products: fully. For third parties: partial.
- **Security mitigations missing from the list?** (4) names only safety mitigations (C5). Security mitigations (C6) are described in the Model Report (M7.3(3)), but not in this detailed on-request list. Perhaps deliberate (security details are themselves sensitive; describing them in detail creates an attack map). A fork should note the absence.
- **10 years after market placement**: long. Matches the AI Act's Art. 18-style retention for high-risk systems (10 years), I believe.
- **"Evidencing adherence"**: the second list (next unit) is about proving compliance, as distinct from describing the model.

**Questions to keep.**
- "What documentation must be kept for the AI Office?" M10.1: detailed architecture; detailed integration into AI systems (insofar as known); detailed evaluations, results and strategies; detailed safety mitigations; retained at least 10 years after market placement; plus adherence evidence (continues).
- Should be asked but might not be: "Are security mitigations in the detailed documentation?" Not in M10.1's first list.

**Prediction for unit 79.** The adherence-evidence list: records of Framework assessments (including non-adherence instances, M1.3), lighter-touch evaluation results at trigger points, records of acceptance determinations and decisions to proceed, incident tracking, external evaluator access/selection, resource allocation, risk-culture surveys. Then M10.2, Public transparency.
