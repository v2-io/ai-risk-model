# Unit 55 · L445–456 · p.21: Measure 7.4, External reports

**What it held.** "Signatories will provide in the Model Report:
1. **any available reports** (e.g. via valid hyperlinks) from: (a) independent external evaluators involved in model evaluations pursuant to Appendix 3.5; and (b) **security reviews undertaken by an independent external party pursuant to Appendix 4.5**; to the extent that respects existing confidentiality (including commercial confidentiality) obligations and **allows such external evaluators or parties to maintain control over the publication of their findings, without implicit endorsement by the Signatories** of the content of such reports;
2. **if no independent external evaluator was involved**…, a justification of how the conditions in **Appendix 3.5, first paragraph, points (1) or (2)** were met; and
3. if at least one independent external evaluator was involved…, an explanation of the choice of evaluator based on the **qualification criteria**."

**What differed.** Predicted: external evaluators' reports and security reviews; justification if none. Not predicted:
- **"Any available reports"**: weaker than I'd guessed. If an evaluator didn't write a report, there's nothing to provide.
- **Evaluators control publication** of their findings, and providers don't implicitly endorse. A provision protecting the evaluator's authorial independence in both directions.
- **External evaluation can legitimately be absent**, if Appendix 3.5's conditions (1) or (2) are met. So pre-market external evaluation isn't always required. The conditions: perhaps "the model is similarly safe or safer" and "no suitable evaluator could be found despite efforts". To be read.
- **Qualification criteria** for evaluators (pre-market), distinct from the post-market selection criteria in M3.5.
- **Appendix 4.5** = an independent external security review.

**Wandering.**
- **Two independence protections.** Evaluators "maintain control over the publication of their findings" (they decide whether and when to publish, separately from the provider) and the provider's inclusion of a report isn't endorsement (so the provider isn't stuck with the evaluator's views, and the evaluator isn't seen as the provider's mouthpiece). That clears up what my unit-53 over-reading reached for: the evaluators' findings enter the Model Report as their own reports, under their control.
- **"To the extent that respects existing confidentiality"**: the evaluator may have agreed to confidentiality (e.g. NDAs on model details). Reports may be redacted for confidentiality, or omitted. Here confidentiality limits what goes *to the AI Office*, which is odd given M1.4's "(unredacted)". Read precisely: it's the *external reports* that are limited by confidentiality obligations (which might bind the evaluators), not the provider's own information. A fork should note this as a potential gap: confidentiality agreements between provider and evaluator could keep evaluator reports from the AI Office.
- **The justification for no external evaluation** is required. So the default is that external evaluation happens, and its absence must be justified against specific conditions. That's a presumption in favour of external evaluation.
- **Appendices are where the operative bite is accumulating**: Appendix 1 (risk taxonomy), 2 (safe reference / similarly safe), 3 (evaluation rigour: 3.2 elicitation, 3.4 internal teams, 3.5 external evaluators), 4 (security: 4.1–4.5, 4.5 external review). I'm reading the body knowing that much of the force lives in the appendices. I'll read them as primary text, not as supplements.

**Questions to keep.**
- "Does the Code require independent external evaluation before release?" By default yes (Appendix 3.5); its absence must be justified in the Model Report against Appendix 3.5's first-paragraph conditions (1) or (2) (M7.4(2)).
- "Do external evaluators control their findings?" Yes: reports are included only in a way that "allows such external evaluators or parties to maintain control over the publication of their findings, without implicit endorsement by the Signatories".
- Should be asked but might not be: "Can confidentiality agreements keep evaluators' reports from the AI Office?" M7.4(1) is limited "to the extent that respects existing confidentiality… obligations".

Then 7.6 update triggers.
