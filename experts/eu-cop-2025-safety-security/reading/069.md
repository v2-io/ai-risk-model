# Unit 69 · L555–559 · p.25: Commitment 9, Serious incident reporting

**What it held.** "**LEGAL TEXT:** Article 55(1), and recitals 114 and 115 AI Act". "Signatories commit to implementing appropriate processes and measures for keeping track of, documenting, and reporting to the AI Office and, **as applicable, to national competent authorities**, without undue delay relevant information about serious incidents **along the entire model lifecycle** and possible corrective measures to address them…. Further, Signatories commit to providing resourcing of such processes and measures appropriate for the severity of the serious incident and **the degree of involvement of their model**."

**What differed.** Predicted the core. Not predicted: **"the degree of involvement of their model"** as a resourcing variable. Incidents vary in how much the model contributed; the response scales with both severity and involvement.

**Wandering.**
- **"Degree of involvement"** acknowledges causal contribution as graded. A model might be the main tool, a minor helper, or merely present. That's the beginning of an attribution theory. I'd expect a definition of "serious incident" that includes some causal language ("directly or indirectly" from recital (j)). For our lexicon: the Code treats model involvement as a degree, not a binary.
- **"Along the entire model lifecycle"**: incidents can happen pre-market (e.g. during internal use, during evaluation: a model exfiltrating itself in testing, or weights stolen during training). So C9 reaches pre-market incidents, unlike the Model Report. That's important: internal-deployment incidents are reportable. A fork should give this as one of the places where internal use *is* covered.
- **"National competent authorities, as applicable"**: incident reporting may go also to Member State authorities (market surveillance? data protection?). The AI Act's GPAI regime is centralised in the AI Office, so "as applicable" probably covers incidents with a national dimension (e.g. critical infrastructure).
- **"Possible corrective measures"**: reports include what's to be done, not only what happened.
- Recital (j) pre-justified this Commitment: pre-incident tracking, not an admission of wrongdoing. I expect 9.1 to require tracking methods before incidents.

**Questions to keep.**
- "Must incidents during internal use or before release be reported?" C9: serious incidents "along the entire model lifecycle".
- "Who receives incident reports?" The AI Office and, as applicable, national competent authorities.

**Prediction for unit 70.** Measure 9.1, Methods for serious incident identification: Signatories will consider the post-market monitoring methods (M3.5) as means of keeping track of incidents; plus review of public sources (press, social media, research), law-enforcement reports, and facilitating reporting by downstream actors, users, and third parties (to the provider or directly to the AI Office).
