# Unit 78 · L138 · p.5

> (b) (1) A large frontier developer shall review and, as appropriate, update its frontier AI framework at least once per year.

## What differed

My recall was exact.

## Wandering

- **"Review and, as appropriate, update"**: review is mandatory annually, and updating isn't ("as appropriate"). A developer can review and decide no change is needed. Nothing has to be published to show the review happened, at least so far. So the annual review is hard to verify from outside. Unless the published framework carries a "last reviewed" date, which isn't required as far as I can see.
- "At least once per year": from when? From first publication, presumably, or calendar year. Unstated.
- (b)(1) and (a)(6) together: (a)(6) describes the developer's own update criteria, and (b)(1) sets an annual floor. The two are compatible.
