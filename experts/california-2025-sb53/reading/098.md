## Wandering

- **Two layers of enforcement for the framework.** Under (a), a large developer must comply with its framework. Under (e)(1)(B), it must not misrepresent its compliance. So there are two separate violations: not complying, and claiming to comply when it doesn't. A lab that fails its framework and says so honestly commits one violation. A lab that fails and says it complied commits two. **That creates an incentive to be honest about noncompliance.**
- "**Implementation of, or compliance with**" matches (a)'s verbs, "implement" and "comply with". Precise drafting.
- The tiers, consistent with the rest: statements about catastrophic risk bind all frontier developers; statements about framework compliance bind only large ones (only they have frameworks).
