# Unit 221 · L308 · p.12

> (e) This act shall not apply to the extent that it is preempted by federal law.

## What differed

It's a federal-preemption acknowledgement, not the local preemption I predicted.

## Wandering

- **Legally redundant, politically pointed.** Federal preemption applies whether the statute says so or not, under the Supremacy Clause. Saying it anyway signals in two directions. To Congress: if you legislate, we yield. To courts: preemption is partial ("to the extent"), so a federal law preempting part of SB 53 leaves the rest intact, which works with severability (5(a)).
- There are now three federal-deference provisions: lawful federal activity (u47), federal contracts (u220) and federal preemption (u221). Plus the OES federal-equivalence mechanism (u120–128) and the Department of Technology's federal-alignment duty (u134). **SB 53 is built to step back as the federal government steps forward**, which was a deliberate posture in 2025, with federal preemption of state AI law being debated (the moratorium fight). Taken together, that's five federal-deference mechanisms in one bill.
- The local preemption is still to come. Three units left.
