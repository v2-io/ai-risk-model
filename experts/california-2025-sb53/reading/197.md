# Unit 197 · L280 · p.11

> (b) A frontier developer shall not enter into a contract that prevents a covered employee from making a disclosure protected under Section 1102.5.

## Wandering

- **This extends the contract bar to existing whistleblower law.** Frontier developers can't contract around 1102.5 for covered employees. 1102.5 already bars employer *policies* that prevent disclosure, but its reach over private contracts (NDAs) is weaker. So (b) closes the contract gap for covered employees' 1102.5 disclosures, which are about violations of any law, not only the TFAIA.
- "**Covered employee**" again. Non-covered employees at the same lab are left to general law. So a frontier developer could still use contracts that restrict 1102.5 disclosures by non-covered staff, insofar as general law allows. That's an odd two-tier workforce: safety staff protected from gag contracts, everyone else less so.
