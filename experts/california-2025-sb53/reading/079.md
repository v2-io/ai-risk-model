# Unit 79 · L139 · p.5

> (2) If a large frontier developer makes a material modification to its frontier AI framework, the large frontier developer shall clearly and conspicuously publish the modified frontier AI framework and a justification for that modification within 30 days.

## What differed

My recall was exact.

## Wandering

- **This partly resolves my unit-69 worry about lowering thresholds.** A material weakening must be published with a *justification* within 30 days. There's no ratchet and no approval; what's required is publicity plus reasons. The check is reputational and evidentiary. A published justification is a statement, and if it's false or misleading it may fall under a false-statement prohibition [recall, to come].
- **"Within 30 days"**: of what? Of making the modification, presumably. So for up to 30 days a developer can operate under a modified framework that hasn't been published. During that window, which version must it "comply with": the published one or the modified one? A gap at the seam. Probably the modified one, since that's "its frontier AI framework".
- **"Material modification"**: materiality again, undefined. A developer decides what's material. A quiet non-material tweak needn't be published or justified. Count of undefined "material(ly)" uses so far:
  - material risk, materially contribute ((c)(1));
  - materially contribute (exclusion (c)(2)(C));
  - materially increased (incident (d)(4));
  - material modifications (compute (i)(2));
  - material modification (here).

  Six. **SB 53 rests on an undefined materiality concept used in at least six places, across risk, causation, compute, incident severity and document change.** Possibly the most load-bearing undefined term in the statute. A question someone should ask: **what does "material" mean in SB 53?** The text gives no definition. Ordinary legal usage (significant enough to matter to a decision) would apply, and it may mean different things in different places.
- Justification is the one place SB 53 requires the developer to give *reasons*, not just describe. "Why did you change your framework?" has to be answered publicly.
