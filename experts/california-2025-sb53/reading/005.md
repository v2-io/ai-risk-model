## Wandering

- This settles the version question as well as the text can: it is the chaptered statute, Chapter 138, Statutes of 2025. A fork citing it can say "SB 53 as chaptered (Stats. 2025, ch. 138)". Whether `bin/canonicalize` or the relata entry records the edition is a different matter; I won't check now, to avoid stepping outside the source.
- The Governor signed it the day it was published. As I remember it, SB 1047 was vetoed in September 2024 and SB 53 signed a year later. A veto message usually travels with a vetoed bill; a signing statement sometimes travels with a signed one. Newsom did issue a signing message for SB 53, I think. It isn't part of the statute, but it's part of the bill's interpretive context. It belongs to a different bounded context (the executive's reading), and the project's corpus might want it.
