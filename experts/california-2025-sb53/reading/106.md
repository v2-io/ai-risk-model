## Wandering

- An internal-use flag on every incident report. This tells OES (and anyone who later aggregates) what share of incidents arise from internal use versus deployment. It's a research variable built into the reporting form: someone anticipated wanting to test the hypothesis that internal deployment is a distinct risk locus. The form asks no other classifying question (no field for incident kind (1)–(4) beyond "reasons", none for model name). Members of the public can rarely know about internal use, so they'll leave it blank or say no. The field is really for developers.
- The four fields: date, qualifying reasons, short statement, internal-use flag. It's a lean form. Notably absent: which model, what mitigations were taken, the number harmed. The "standardized" promise of finding (l) is met minimally.
