# Unit 200 · L283 · p.11

> (1) At all times posting and displaying within any workplace maintained by the frontier developer a notice to all covered employees of their rights under this section, ensuring that any new covered employee receives equivalent notice, and ensuring that any covered employee who works remotely periodically receives an equivalent notice.

## What differed

My prediction was nearly verbatim. That's an odd thing for an employment-notice provision, unless it copies a standard template.

## Wandering

- **"Rights under this section"**: the notice is about rights, while (d)'s lead-in said "rights and responsibilities". A small internal inconsistency.
- **"Any workplace maintained by the frontier developer"** includes workplaces outside California? The Labor Code applies to employment in California. A frontier developer with a London office: does the posting duty reach it? Probably not. California employment law generally reaches California-based work. **A question someone should ask: are covered employees outside California protected?** Probably not, apart from remote workers *in* California. The text doesn't address territoriality, and general Labor Code territorial principles would govern.
- "Works remotely" is a post-2020 drafting touch. The template includes remote-worker notice.
