## Wandering

- The wording hedges twice: "reasonable efforts" and "to the extent possible". The UC preference is soft. UC is constitutionally autonomous in California (Art. IX, §9), so the Legislature can't simply direct UC to host it. It can only ask the consortium to try. The hedging probably reflects that constitutional reality, not timidity.
- "**Established**": the consortium plans for CalCompute to be *established* within UC. That's the first hint of what CalCompute would be institutionally: a UC-hosted resource, like the UC-run national labs.
