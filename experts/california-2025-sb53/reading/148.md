## Wandering

- **"Safe" here has a different meaning from the TFAIA's.** "AI that is safe, ethical, equitable, and sustainable" is the aspirational register, with no definitions. "Safe" isn't tied to catastrophic risk or critical safety incidents, so it's general safety. The lexicon note from u12 stands, now in operative text: "safe" in §11546.8 ≠ "safety" in "critical safety incident".
- **The modifier attaches ambiguously.** "A framework for the creation of CalCompute that advances …": what advances AI, the framework or CalCompute? The intent is CalCompute, which advances AI development by providing compute. Grammatically either reading works. No legal stakes, but it's typical of how SB 53's relative clauses attach loosely.
- "**At a minimum**" sets a floor, so the consortium can do more.
- Development *and* deployment: public compute for deploying AI, not just research.
