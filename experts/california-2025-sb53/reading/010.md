# Unit 10 · L42 · p.2

> The TFAIA would impose a civil penalty for noncompliance with the TFAIA to be enforced by the Attorney General, as prescribed.

## What differed

My second prediction, but much barer than I'd recalled. There's no dollar figure and no "per violation", only "as prescribed". The digest leaves the amount out.

## Wandering

- "Noncompliance with the TFAIA" is broad, and it bears on the open question from unit 8: if compliance with one's own framework is a TFAIA requirement, then failing to follow your framework is "noncompliance with the TFAIA". The digest doesn't settle it. The statute will.
- Enforcement by the Attorney General only, as far as the digest says. No private right of action under the B&P part. That is a design choice: SB 1047 also relied on AG enforcement, I think. The whistleblower part in the Labor Code will probably have its own remedies (civil action by the employee, injunctive relief). So **who can sue whom** differs between the parts of the bill, as I suspected in unit 4.
- The page break at L38→L42 (lines 39–41 skipped) is the canonical text's page marker. "p.2" means a short first page of chrome and front matter.
- I notice the digest's sentences get shorter here. The PRA and penalty lines are one-liners, after a 200-word sentence on frameworks and OES. The weight of the digest's attention, and probably of the bill's, sits on the framework and reporting mechanisms.
