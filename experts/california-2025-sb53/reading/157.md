# Unit 157 · L230 · p.9

> (f) (1) On or before January 1, 2027, the Government Operations Agency shall submit, pursuant to Section 9795, a report from the consortium to the Legislature with the framework developed pursuant to subdivision (b) for the creation and operation of CalCompute.

## What differed

My recall was exact. "Section 9795" appears bare again, as in u119. In a Government Code section, a bare "Section 9795" is unambiguous, because §9795 is itself in the Government Code. In the B&P chapter it was ambiguous. So the bare form is correct here.

## Wandering

- **GovOps submits; the consortium writes.** The report is "from the consortium", submitted by the agency. The agency is the conduit.
- "**For the creation and operation of CalCompute**": the framework covers operation as well as creation, so governance and use too. The report's required contents are probably listed in (f)(2).
- **The date is 2027-01-01, conditional on appropriation.** If the appropriation comes in mid-2026, the consortium has six months. If it comes after January 1, 2027, the deadline has already passed before the duty begins. The statute doesn't resolve this. Recall from u12 that the operative-on-appropriation clause is still to come, probably in (h) or (i).
