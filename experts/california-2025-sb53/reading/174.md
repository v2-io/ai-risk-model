## Wandering

- **The donation clause can't work without state money.** "*This section*", all of §11546.8, including (j)'s private-donation pathway, is inoperative until there's a *state* appropriation. So even if a philanthropist offered to fund CalCompute entirely, **this section** wouldn't be operative without the Legislature appropriating something. The donation pathway is gated behind public money. Whether a token appropriation would do ("or other measure") is open. So the honest summary is: **all of CalCompute, including private funding under this section, waits on a state appropriation.**
- "Or other measure" means a separate bill could appropriate, not only the budget act.
- **CalCompute's whole status:** established on paper; operative only on appropriation; a 14-member unpaid consortium; one report due 2027-01-01; dissolves on submission; a UC-hosting preference; publicly owned infrastructure in the plan; donations allowed once operative. As of the bill's signing it is **legally dormant**. Did the 2025–26 budget appropriate anything? I don't know, and the statute can't say. Someone should check.
- SEC. 3 is done. Next is probably SEC. 4, the Labor Code chapter.
