## Wandering

- "**CalCompute shall include**" is an odd duty. CalCompute doesn't exist; the consortium only develops a framework for creating it. So this "shall" binds a future object. It's practically a design requirement for the framework: whatever the framework plans must include the following. It's another category slip of the kind I've been collecting, a duty addressed to a hypothetical thing. In substance it means "the framework shall provide that CalCompute includes …".
