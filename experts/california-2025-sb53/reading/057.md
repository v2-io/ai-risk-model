## Wandering

- "Broad" is undefined. A model trained on a broad biomedical corpus (all of PubMed plus protein databases) — is that a "broad data set"? Broad within a domain, narrow across domains. Specialized biological design tools (protein-design models like the ESM family) are exactly the models some bio-risk experts worry about most, and the "broad data set" + "generality of output" conditions may **exclude specialized biological AI tools from SB 53 altogether**, even above the compute threshold, though few such tools reach 10^26. A question someone should ask: **does SB 53 cover narrow but dangerous models, such as biological design tools?** Probably not, via the foundation-model conditions. The (n) finding's admitted gap ("smaller companies or … behind the frontier") doesn't even mention this kind of exclusion: narrow models are outside by *kind*, not by size.
- "Data set" is singular. Training is on many datasets mixed, but the singular here means the aggregate.
