## What differed

**The TFAIA ends at §22757.16.** The chapter has seven sections:

- 22757.10: title;
- 22757.11: definitions;
- 22757.12: obligations;
- 22757.13: OES;

It contains no preemption clause, no liberal-construction clause, and no severability clause.

## Wandering

- **Where are preemption and liberal construction?** The digest promised local preemption (u16), and CLAUDE.md quotes "liberally construed to effectuate its purposes". Neither is in the TFAIA chapter. So they're in uncodified bill sections (SEC. 4+), or in the Labor Code chapter. If preemption sits in an uncodified section, it applies to "this act" or names the chapter. If liberal construction is in the Labor Code chapter only, then **the liberal-construction rule may apply to the whistleblower provisions, not the TFAIA.** That would matter: CLAUDE.md's "SB 53 is to be 'liberally construed to effectuate its purposes'" would be true of part of SB 53 only. Strong watch item.
- The TFAIA, then, as a whole: definitions, obligations, two OES mechanisms, a review, penalties, and a one-line exclusion. About 80 units of the 224. The rest is findings, digest, CalCompute, the Labor Code chapter, and closing sections.
- Now CalCompute (SEC. 3, Government Code §11546.8). It has no finding in SEC. 1 (u37), so the section may carry its own purpose language. The register should shift from the threshold vocabulary to the aspirational one ("safe, ethical, equitable, and sustainable").
