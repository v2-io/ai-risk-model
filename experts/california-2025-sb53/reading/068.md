## Wandering

- **Combining "comply with" and "describes how … approaches" produces a recursion.** The framework must describe how the developer approaches *incorporating standards into the framework*. A framework that says "we approach incorporating standards by reviewing NIST AI RMF annually and adopting what we judge relevant" satisfies (1). It needn't actually incorporate anything specific. And it must *comply* with that description. So the obligation amounts to: say how you'll use standards, then do what you said.
- Which standards? None are named: no NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, or Frontier Model Forum. "National" (US, presumably NIST and CAISI guidance), "international" (ISO, maybe the Seoul commitments or OECD), "industry-consensus best practices" (the Frontier Model Forum's issue briefs?). The lack of a list means the statute ages well, and the lack of a floor means it asks nothing specific.
- "Industry-consensus best practices" again treats industry consensus as a legitimate source of standards. Same theory as finding (e).
