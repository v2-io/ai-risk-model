## Wandering

- **"A natural person"** is the first time SB 53 says *natural* person. So the statute knows the difference between natural persons and persons, which makes the undefined "person" in (a) and (h) more clearly the broader legal sense (including entities). That supports unit 66: a frontier developer is a "person", which can be an entity or (by not being restricted to entities) an individual. Here a *human* must be able to reach the developer.
- Why "natural person"? Probably to require a human-reachable channel, not a bot-only or API-only contact. Perhaps also to say that the *communicator* must be a natural person, so that AI agents contacting the lab aren't who the channel is for. In 2025 that distinction began to matter. A small sign of the drafters thinking about agents.
- It's the contact point for public reports? Members of the public report critical safety incidents to OES (digest, u8), not to the developer. So this mechanism is general contact, not incident intake.
