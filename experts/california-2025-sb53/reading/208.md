# Unit 208 · L291 · p.11

> (2) Upon the filing of the petition for injunctive relief, the petitioner shall cause notice thereof to be served upon the person, and thereupon the court shall have jurisdiction to grant temporary injunctive relief as the court deems just and proper.

## What differed

As predicted, verbatim. It's procedural boilerplate from 1102.61.

## Wandering

- "**Served upon the person**": "the person" again, with no antecedent in this subdivision. The template's "person" (the employer, in 1102.61) wasn't adapted to "frontier developer". It's another template artifact, like u206's "proscribed". The Labor Code chapter is assembled from (a) TFAIA definitions, copied and adapted, and (b) Labor Code 1102.x remedies templates, copied with less adaptation. **You can see the two drafting sources in the vocabulary**: "frontier developer" in the adapted parts, "the person" in the template parts.
