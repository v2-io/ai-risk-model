# Unit 139 · L209 · p.8

> (c) Upon developing recommendations pursuant to this section, the Department of Technology shall submit a report to the Legislature, pursuant to Section 9795 of the Government Code, with those recommendations.

## What differed

It's "**upon developing recommendations**", not "upon appropriation". So the review isn't conditional on funding, unlike CalCompute.

## Wandering

- "Upon developing recommendations" contains a loop: (a) requires the Department to "make recommendations about whether and how to update". A recommendation could be "no update needed". Is that "developing recommendations"? Yes, presumably. So an annual report goes to the Legislature every year from 2027.
- "Section 9795 **of the Government Code**". Here the code is named; in §22757.13(g)(3) (u119) it was "Section 9795" bare. That one was ambiguous in a B&P chapter, since a bare section number would normally refer to the same code, and B&P has no §9795 that fits. It's an internal inconsistency, and the bare one is the drafting slip. Small, but this is a statute where cross-references matter, and the bare reference is formally ambiguous.
- So annual reports to the Legislature now come from two places: OES (incidents) and the Department of Technology (definitions). The Legislature is the endpoint of every feedback loop.
