- The digest has said nothing about **definitions**: no thresholds, no ">50 people or $1B", no 10^26. It's said "as defined" five or six times without giving one. For a summarizer, that's sensible. A ranker that favours keyword density could put the digest on top for exactly the queries it can't answer. **That's a concrete thing to tell the search tool**, since the reordering task is one of my future jobs.
