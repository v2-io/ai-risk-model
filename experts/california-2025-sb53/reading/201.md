## What differed

## Wandering

- "Received and acknowledged" is a signature requirement, which helps evidentially later: an employee can't be told they didn't know their rights.
- **Telling the developer who its covered employees are.** The notice duty, in either form, requires the developer to identify its covered employees, the ones responsible for critical-safety-incident risk. So the developer decides who's covered when it decides whom to notify. An employee left off the notice list could argue they're covered anyway, since the definition is functional, not list-based. But the list carries practical weight, and it's another quasi-self-set parameter.
