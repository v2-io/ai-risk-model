## Wandering

- The reporter must classify the incident under (d)(1)–(4) and say why. So **every report is also an interpretation of the incident definition**. Over time, OES's collection of reports will be a corpus of developers' and the public's readings of what "loss of control", "materialization" and "materially increased catastrophic risk" mean. If those reports were ever published, even aggregated, they would be the best evidence of how the definition works in practice. They're PRA-exempt, though (digest, u9). So the interpretive record stays sealed.
