## Wandering

- **Scope of the name.** "*This chapter* shall be known as the TFAIA". So the TFAIA is **only B&P Chapter 25.1**. CalCompute (the Government Code) and the whistleblower chapter (the Labor Code) are *not* the TFAIA, though they're in the same bill. "SB 53" ≠ "the TFAIA". The digest blurred this: it described the whistleblower protections in item (3), separately, yet unit 14's prong (b) was "violated the TFAIA", which refers into the B&P chapter. So the Labor Code chapter cross-references the TFAIA as another statute. **A crisp distinction a fork should keep: SB 53 (the bill, three codes) ⊃ TFAIA (B&P Ch. 25.1 only).** Sources in the corpus that say "the TFAIA requires whistleblower protections" are slightly wrong. "SB 53 requires" is right.
