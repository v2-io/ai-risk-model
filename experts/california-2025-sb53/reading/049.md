## Wandering

- The defined terms go in order: Affiliate, Artificial intelligence model, Catastrophic risk, Critical safety incident. Alphabetical, so after this probably: Deploy, Foundation model, Frontier AI framework, Frontier developer, Frontier model, Large frontier developer, Model weight, Person, Property?, Safety incident?…
- "Means any of the following" is a closed enumerated list again, like catastrophic risk's scenarios. So "critical safety incident" is an enumerated *kind*, not a general concept. Anything that isn't on the list isn't a critical safety incident, however alarming. That matters for reporting duties: a developer that discovers something alarming but off-list has no duty to report it as a critical safety incident.
- The incident concept is what turns a risk into an event. Catastrophic risk is a risk; critical safety incident is an event. I expect one item to bridge them: "harm resulting from the materialization of a catastrophic risk". That's probably one of the "two other provisions" the catastrophic-risk definition reaches into.
