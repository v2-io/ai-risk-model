## Wandering

- "To **ensure** implementation" is the strongest verb in the list. But it's the purpose of the governance practices, not a guarantee the statute imposes. The developer describes governance practices meant to ensure implementation.
- "These processes" refers back to items (1)–(8). So governance is meta: it watches over the rest. In lab frameworks this is the board-level oversight, the Responsible Scaling Officer, or the Safety Advisory Group. SB 53 doesn't require any particular structure (no independent board committee, no named officer), only a description of practices.
- Pattern across the list so far: every item names a *topic*, none names a *standard*. That's consistent with the opening verb, "describes how [it] approaches". Read whole, §22757.12(a) is a **mandatory table of contents** for a document whose content the developer writes, plus an enforceable promise to follow what it writes.
