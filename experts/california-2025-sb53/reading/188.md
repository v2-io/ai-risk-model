# Unit 188 · L268 · p.10

> (2) Harm resulting from the materialization of a catastrophic risk.

## What differed

The words are identical to TFAIA (d)(2). But "catastrophic risk" here refers to §1107(a), the foundation-model version, so the item reaches further than it does in the TFAIA.

## Wandering

- Identical wording with a different referent. That's a pure instance of **meaning carried by bounded context**: the same string means different things in two chapters of one bill, because each chapter's defined terms resolve locally. A search hit on this sentence has to carry its chapter, or it can't be interpreted. **For retrieval and tagging, every SB 53 passage needs its code location (B&P or Labor), because identical text resolves differently.** This is the most concrete case so far for the project's bounded-context design applying *within* a source.
- The materialization ambiguity from u51 carries over unchanged.
