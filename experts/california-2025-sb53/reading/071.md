## Wandering

- **"Use it extensively internally"** is new vocabulary. Internal use gets a decision point equivalent to deployment, but only *extensive* internal use. Light internal use (a few researchers) doesn't trigger review under (4); extensive internal use (company-wide agents, automated AI R&D) does. "Extensively" is undefined. That's the internal-deployment concern (AI labs using their own frontier models at scale before release) reaching the operative text. Compare the digest's "internal use" (u8) and the catastrophic-risk conduct "use" (u41). **Three internal-use phrasings now: "use" (c)(1), "internal use" (digest), "use it extensively internally" (here).** Watch for "internal use" as an operative phrase in the OES summaries section.
- "**Adequacy of mitigations**" is reviewed, but against what standard? The framework describes how the developer *reviews* adequacy. It doesn't say mitigations must be adequate. Still no adequacy standard from the state; that's the pattern.
- "As part of the decision to deploy": it describes the *go/no-go decision process*. Labs' frameworks have this (Anthropic's Responsible Scaling Officer, OpenAI's Safety Advisory Group). So item (4) asks the developer to describe its release governance.
