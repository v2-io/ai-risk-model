## Wandering

- "**Shall strongly consider**" is an obligation to deliberate, not to reach a result. It's a soft-hard mixed norm: mandatory to consider, with no outcome required. "Strongly" is an intensifier with no legal content, really. It's another speech-act type for the inventory: a **deliberative duty**.
- The four considerations echo the redaction grounds in §22757.12(f)(1): trade secrets, cybersecurity, public safety, national security. (Here "compliance with law" is missing, which is natural, since transmission happens under law.) So the same four interests guard both the developer's public documents and the state's onward transmissions. **They are the statute's standing reasons for secrecy, and they will probably reappear in the PRA findings section.** That's a consistent "confidentiality value set" across the bill.
