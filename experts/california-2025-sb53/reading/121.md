# Unit 121 · L188 · p.7

> (1) (A) The law, regulation, or guidance document imposes or states standards or requirements for critical safety incident reporting that are substantially equivalent to, or stricter than, those required by this section.

## What differed

As predicted, though the structure is "(1) (A)", so (B) follows within (1).

## Wandering

- "**Imposes or states**": "states" is what lets a guidance document qualify. It *states* standards without imposing them. So the equivalence test is about the content of the standard, not its bindingness.
- "**Substantially equivalent to, or stricter than**": who judges? OES, in rulemaking. "Substantially equivalent" is a third undefined qualifier family (after material and substantially modified). It's the standard formula for reciprocity in regulatory law.
- **"Critical safety incident reporting"** in the federal document has to be equivalent. Does the federal standard have to use SB 53's definition of critical safety incident? "Substantially equivalent" requirements could define incidents differently and still be equivalent overall. A real translation problem for OES: comparing two bounded contexts' incident concepts. Exactly this project's problem, inside a state agency's rulemaking.
