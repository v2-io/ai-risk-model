## Wandering

- **There's no safety screening for CalCompute users.** "Which users and projects will be supported" is an allocation question. Would CalCompute screen projects for dual-use risk? The text doesn't say. A public cluster that trains models for any Californian researcher could, in principle, host dangerous work, and the TFAIA wouldn't reach it unless the project crossed 10^26. The bill doesn't connect its two halves here. The TFAIA's catastrophic-risk concepts don't appear in CalCompute's parameters for use.
- That disconnection is a structural observation worth recording. **SB 53's two main parts don't reference each other.** The TFAIA doesn't mention CalCompute, and CalCompute doesn't mention catastrophic risk. They're two policies in one bill, linked only by the findings' general purposes (and CalCompute has no finding of its own).
