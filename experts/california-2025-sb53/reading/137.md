# Unit 137 · L207 · p.8

> (4) The complexity of determining whether a person or foundation model is covered, with an aim toward allowing simpler determinations if possible.

## What differed

A fourth consideration I hadn't recalled: **simplicity**.

## Wandering

- **Simplicity is a stated value.** Together with (3) (predictability), the review must favour simple, early determinations. That's a strong pull toward bright lines (numbers) and away from qualitative tests ("at the frontier", "well-resourced"). So the review section asks for definitions that (a) track the frontier and well-resourced developers, and (b) are simple and predictable. Those goals conflict, and the statute admits it implicitly with "if possible" twice.
- "**A person or foundation model is covered**": coverage is by person (frontier developer, large frontier developer) or by model (frontier model). The two-axis structure again (u34).
- Thinking about this project: the same tension (track the phenomenon vs. keep the test simple) exists in our own lexicon design. A definition precise enough to capture what a source means is often too complex to apply mechanically. The review section is the Legislature's version of our problem.
