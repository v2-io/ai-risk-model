## Wandering

- The subject line is "large developers", not "frontier developers". On leginfo the subject is the measure's digest-key subject line, and it often stays as introduced even after the bill's content changes. If SB 53 began life as something else (I vaguely recall an early SB 53 being mainly the CalCompute bill, with whistleblower protections), then the title is fossil vocabulary. "Large developer" may also be a term the final text actually uses (the "large frontier developer" tier). A reader shouldn't take the title as the operative term. Possible name drift to watch: "large developer" in the title against "large frontier developer" or "frontier developer" in the body. That's exactly the "one word, many things" problem this project exists to catch, inside a single document.
- "Date Published" is not "date enacted" and not "effective date". Statutes enacted in a regular session in California generally take effect January 1 of the following year, so January 1, 2026, unless there's an urgency clause, which I doubt. Any operative dates inside the text (reporting obligations starting at some date, a CalCompute report due by some date) are different again. That gives at least four dates: published, approved by the Governor, filed with the Secretary of State, operative. A fork asked "when did SB 53 take effect?" should keep them apart.
- "SHARE THIS:" is more chrome mixed into the same unit as real metadata. The reading tool joined a heading to the paragraph after it, as designed, and the paragraph after happened to be junk-plus-date. Not a problem, but the units won't always be clean semantic units.
- The bold markers (`**`) survived from the HTML. If bold carries meaning later (leginfo bolds section numbers, and sometimes defined terms in digests), I may be able to use it. Struck-through or italicized amendment text, the way leginfo shows amended-version changes, would be a problem in a web capture. A chaptered version shouldn't have them. If I see italic runs mixed with plain runs, that means amendment markup and a different version.
