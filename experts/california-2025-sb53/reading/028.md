## Wandering

- Whistleblower protections are framed as instruments **of transparency**, not of safety or justice. The bill's single organizing value is transparency, and every mechanism is justified as serving it. That's a coherent design: one value, several instruments. It also means that under the liberal-construction clause, ambiguities would be resolved toward *more information flow*. That's a real, predictable interpretive tilt. For example, an ambiguous scope of "covered employee" would be read broadly, if a court follows the findings.
- "Public-facing information sharing": the frameworks and transparency reports. Paired with whistleblowers, it shows the two directions information flows: *outward to the public* (published documents) and *upward to authorities* (whistleblowers, incident reports). This finding doesn't mention incident reporting to government. Maybe the next one does.
