## Wandering

- The pattern is now clear: **the Labor Code chapter restates its definitions in full instead of cross-referencing the TFAIA.** That's probably deliberate. A Labor Code chapter enforced through employment law, with its own litigants (employees), avoids depending on a B&P chapter that someone might later amend. The cost is drift: two definitions that can evolve separately under future amendments. That's already begun with the stylistic drift (u179, u184).
- If the items say "foundation model", the covered-employee mismatch in u185 disappears: both point at foundation models.
