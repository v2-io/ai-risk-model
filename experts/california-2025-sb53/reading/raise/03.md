## Wandering

- **"Wildfire forecasting and prevention" in a New York finding.** California's state-specific benefit (u22) has been carried, unedited, into New York's findings. It's the clearest copy fingerprint you could want, the way a misprint gives away a copied map. Wildfire isn't irrelevant to New York (there are the Adirondacks, and Canadian smoke in 2023), but it would never have been an NY drafter's first example. **This is evidence of verbatim derivation**, quotable for the lineage map: RAISE (S8828) §1 ← SB 53 SEC. 1.
- **What NY dropped is informative.**
  - The JCPWG reference makes sense to drop, since it's California's report.
  - "Balance … benefits and … material risks" (d) is dropped. That was the proportionality finding, and dropping it removes some of the light-touch rationale.
  - The **harm specifics in (j)** are dropped: no malicious use or malfunction categories, no hacking, bio or loss of control. NY's finding says only "catastrophic risks". So the IASR-derived risk taxonomy (u30) doesn't pass into NY's findings. **The lineage edge SB 53 → IASR categories doesn't carry over to RAISE**, at least here.
- "**Align incentives**" is dropped too. Maybe it read as jargon, or seemed a promise the bill doesn't keep.
- "Facilitate understanding and monitoring" is a mild expansion: incident reporting is also for *understanding*.
- **Conversion artifacts**: line numbers inlined ("1 Section 1 … 2 declares"), hyphenation splits ("develop- 8 ment", "fron- 18 tier"), "Assem bly", and the "EXPLANATION--Matter in italics … is new; matter in brackets … old law" boilerplate with a document code ("LBD00047-16-6"). NY bill PDFs number every line. **For search: words broken by hyphens and line numbers will defeat exact-phrase matching here** ("frontier AI capabilities" appears as "fron- 18 tier AI capabilities"). A canonicalization issue to report: NY's line-number-plus-hyphen layout needs its own cleaning.
- "**Legislative findings and intent**": the heading promises intent, and SB 53's (p) intent clause may come.
