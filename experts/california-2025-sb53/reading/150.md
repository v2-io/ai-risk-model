## Wandering

- "Equitable" appears twice: the AI is equitable ((b)), and so is the innovation ((b)(2)). The equity aim here is access, democratizing compute against its concentration in a few firms. That's the counterweight to the TFAIA: the TFAIA regulates the few large labs, and CalCompute widens who else can build. Seen as one bill, SB 53 tries both to constrain the frontier and to broaden the base. Finding (g)'s "competition" purpose (u27) fits CalCompute better than the TFAIA.
