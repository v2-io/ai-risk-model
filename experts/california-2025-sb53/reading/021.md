# Unit 21 · L64–66 · p.2

> SECTION 1. The Legislature finds and declares all of the following:
> (a) California is leading the world in artificial intelligence innovation and research through companies large and small and through the state's remarkable public and private universities.

## What differed

My second prediction (findings), not the first. The short title isn't in SEC. 1, so it's probably in §22757.10. The findings open on California's leadership, not on risk.

## Wandering

- Finding (a) is boosterish: "leading the world", "remarkable", "companies large and small". The first thing the Legislature declares is innovation, not danger. That's the political posture of SB 53 compared with SB 1047: lead with innovation, then justify light-touch transparency. Findings aren't operative, but courts read them for purpose, and CLAUDE.md said SB 53 is to be "liberally construed to effectuate its purposes". **If there's a liberal-construction clause, the findings tell you what the purposes are.** So these findings are less decorative than they look. "Liberally construe to effectuate innovation-and-safety purposes" is a different instruction from "… to effectuate safety purposes". I'll watch which purposes the findings name, and whether the construction clause points at them.
- "Companies large and small" anticipates the tiering: the bill will burden the large ones more and want the small ones to feel safe. "Public and private universities" anticipates CalCompute's consortium and its researcher beneficiaries.
- There's no risk vocabulary yet. I expect the findings to build: (a) leadership, (b) AI's promise, (c) its risks, (d) the JCPWG report, (e) transparency as the tool, (f) whistleblowers, (g) CalCompute.
