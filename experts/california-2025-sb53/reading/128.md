## Wandering

- **Designation is discretionary; revocation is mandatory.** OES *may* designate under (h), and *shall* revoke under (j) once the conditions fail. So the asymmetry protects California's standards. Entering equivalence is optional, but staying in it requires that equivalence continue.
- Practically, OES must monitor federal standards over time. Who triggers review? No mechanism is given. Revocation duties without monitoring duties rely on someone noticing.
- I suspect §22757.13 ends soon. Remaining TFAIA pieces I recall: enforcement and civil penalties (§22757.15?), the Department of Technology's annual definitions review (§22757.14?), and a severability or non-preemption clause. Also the preemption of local law, somewhere.
