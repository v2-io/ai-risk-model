# Unit 207 · L290 · p.11

> (h) (1) In a civil action or administrative proceeding brought pursuant to this section, a covered employee may petition the superior court in any county wherein the violation in question is alleged to have occurred, or wherein the person resides or transacts business, for appropriate temporary or preliminary injunctive relief.

## What differed

As predicted. "**Or administrative proceeding**" is new: there's an administrative route too, the Labor Commissioner, presumably.

## Wandering

- **Injunctive relief.** A covered employee facing retaliation can get a temporary or preliminary injunction (reinstatement, a stop to the retaliation) quickly, while the case proceeds. For safety staff, getting back inside the lab may matter more than damages.
- "**Wherein the person resides or transacts business**": "the person" is the frontier developer presumably, though it's grammatically unclear. "Person" is the undefined term again, now in the Labor Code. It fixes venue: any California county where the developer does business. Labs in San Francisco would face suits in SF or wherever else they transact.
- "**Administrative proceeding**": a Labor Commissioner complaint (Labor Code §98.7) is the usual route for 1102.5 claims. So covered employees can go administrative or judicial. That's a familiar structure, unremarkable except that it confirms the chapter plugs into existing machinery.
