# Unit 204 · L287 · p.11

> (B) If a covered employee has alleged wrongdoing by an officer or director of the large frontier developer in a disclosure or response, subparagraph (A) shall not apply with respect to that officer or director.

## What differed

As predicted, verbatim.

## Wandering

- **The accused don't see the accusation.** It's sensible conflict-of-interest design. But consider a CEO who is also a director, accused of overriding safety processes. The CEO is excluded, and the other directors still receive the report. That works only if the board has members independent of the accused, which is the governance-structure question that some 2023–25 lab controversies turned on.
- "**In a disclosure or response**": wrongdoing alleged in the developer's *response* too? That would cover a disclosure investigated and answered by an officer who is then accused. It's a little odd, but it closes a loophole.
