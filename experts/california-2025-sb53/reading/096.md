## Wandering

- **Another self-set parameter.** "Every three months **or pursuant to another reasonable schedule specified by the large frontier developer**". The quarterly default can be replaced by the developer's own schedule, so long as it's "reasonable". Who judges reasonableness? Presumably OES or the AG, after the fact. The self-definition pattern continues: scope (primary purpose, design), reporting triggers (substantially modified), framework thresholds, and now cadence.
  - Running list of **parameters the developer sets for itself**: deploy's "primary purpose"; foundation model's "designed for"; framework thresholds; update criteria; "substantially modified"; what counts as a "material modification"; the internal-use reporting schedule.
- "**A summary of any assessment**": if the developer conducts no internal-use assessment in a quarter, there's nothing to summarize. Does "any" mean "every assessment that occurred" (possibly none)? Yes. So the duty is conditional on assessments existing. But the framework (a)(10) requires describing how internal-use risk is *assessed*, and the developer must comply. So if its framework says it assesses quarterly, it must, and then send the summary. The two provisions interlock through "comply with".
- "**With written updates, as appropriate**": updates to the schedule, presumably. Ambiguous whether "written updates" are updates to the schedule or to the summaries. The grammar ("specified … and communicated in writing … with written updates, as appropriate") attaches the updates to the schedule communication.
- **This is the confidential channel**: OES gets it, PRA-exempt (digest, u9). So the state learns about internal-use risks, in summary, and the public doesn't.
- The digest's typo (u9: "internet use") becomes more conspicuous. The operative text says "internal use", plainly.
