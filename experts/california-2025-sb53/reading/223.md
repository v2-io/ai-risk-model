> SEC. 6. ## What differed

It's the PRA findings, as predicted at u9 and u17. The section names **only Section 2 (the TFAIA)**, not the Labor Code chapter.

## Wandering

- **Why only the TFAIA?** The PRA exemption itself sits in the TFAIA, at §22757.13(f), even though it covers covered-employee reports made under the Labor Code (u116). So the limitation is imposed by Section 2, and naming only Section 2 is accurate. The structure is coherent: the exemption lives in one place and reaches reports generated under both chapters.
- It's the constitutional form exactly: a finding that a limitation exists, then findings on the interest protected and why protection is needed. The next unit, the last, should state the interest. **It's the one place SB 53 says in its own voice why secrecy serves the public.**
