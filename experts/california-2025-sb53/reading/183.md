## Wandering

- This has an odd consequence in the whistleblower context. An employee who reasonably believes the lab's work for a federal customer poses a specific and substantial danger has no "catastrophic risk" to report if that federal activity is lawful. Prong (a) is unavailable, and only prong (b), a TFAIA violation, remains. So **whistleblowing about the lawful federal uses of a frontier lab's models isn't protected under the catastrophic-danger prong.** Employees working on defence or intelligence contracts would rely on federal whistleblower law instead, which has its own channels.
