## Wandering

- "Summaries of … assessments … conducted pursuant to the … framework". The report summarizes assessments *the framework calls for*. If the framework calls for few assessments, there's little to summarize. Everything flows from the developer's own framework, which is self-referential all the way down. The quality of a large developer's transparency report is bounded by the ambition of its framework.
- "Catastrophic risks **from the frontier model**": risks from this model specifically, not general.
