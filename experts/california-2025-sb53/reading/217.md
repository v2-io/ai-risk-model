# Unit 217 · L304 · p.12

> SEC. 5. (a) The provisions of this act are severable. If any provision of this act or its application is held invalid, that invalidity shall not affect other provisions or applications that can be given effect without the invalid provision or application.

## What differed

SEC. 5 is severability, as (a). So SEC. 5 has subdivisions, and preemption is probably (b).

## Wandering

- **Severability for a bill that expects challenges.** Severability is boilerplate, but for this bill it matters. The parts most likely to be challenged:
  - the false-statement rule (First Amendment, u97);
  - preemption, if a city litigates;
  - federal preemption of the whole (if Congress or the executive acts);
  - CalCompute's appointments (separation of powers, u166).

  Severability keeps the rest alive if one falls.
- "**Applications**": as-applied invalidity is severable too. For example, if the false-statement rule were invalid as applied to opinion statements, it would survive for factual ones.
