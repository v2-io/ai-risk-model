# Unit 124 · L191 · p.7

> (i) (1) A frontier developer that intends to comply with this section by complying with the requirements of, or meeting the standards stated by, a federal law, regulation, or guidance document designated pursuant to subdivision (h) shall declare its intent to do so to the Office of Emergency Services.

## Wandering

- **Opting in to federal equivalence takes a declaration.** The developer must tell OES it intends to rely on the federal standard. So OES at least knows who has opted out of direct reporting. That softens my u122 worry. OES won't get the reports, but it will know who reports elsewhere.
- "**Complying with the requirements of, or meeting the standards stated by**" mirrors (h)(1)(A)'s "imposes or states". A binding federal law is *complied with*; a guidance document's standards are *met*. Careful, parallel drafting.
- "**Comply with this section**" means all of §22757.13, so presumably only the frontier developer's duties in it (incident reporting). The internal-use summaries are under §22757.12(d), a different section, so federal equivalence doesn't reach them. Large developers still send internal-use summaries to OES. Good to know: **federal equivalence covers incident reporting, not internal-use summaries.**
