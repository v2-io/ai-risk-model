## Wandering

- The framework must describe incident *identification* and *response*, while *reporting* is a separate statutory duty (to OES, later). So the incident regime has two layers: what the developer says it does internally (the framework) and what the statute requires it to tell the state (the report). A framework could also commit to identifying events *beyond* the four defined incident kinds, and then be bound by that commitment. Since the definition is closed, any broader internal monitoring is voluntary until written into the framework, after which it's enforceable.
- This is a short item, which fits: the bill's incident details live in the reporting section.
