# Unit 107 · L171 · p.6

> (b) (1) The Office of Emergency Services shall establish a mechanism to be used by a large frontier developer to confidentially submit summaries of any assessments of the potential for catastrophic risk resulting from internal use of its frontier models.

## What differed

The order differed from my recall. Subdivision (b) is the OES *receiving* mechanism for internal-use summaries, not the 15-day reporting duty.

## Wandering

- **The paired provisions word the same object differently.** On the sender side (§22757.12(d), u96) the object is a "summary of any **assessment of catastrophic risk** resulting from internal use". On the receiver side (here) it's "summaries of any **assessments of the potential for catastrophic risk** resulting from internal use". Same object, two wordings, in two sections that are plainly meant to mesh. That confirms unit 70's lexicon note from inside the operative text: **"potential for catastrophic risk" and "catastrophic risk" are interchangeable idioms in SB 53.** An extractor shouldn't produce two concepts. The digest showed the same variation (u8), so it came from the bill's own drafting.
- "**Confidentially**": the word appears for the first time in operative text. The PRA exemption will make it legally binding.
- "(b) (1)" means more follows: maybe a paragraph on what OES does with the summaries.
