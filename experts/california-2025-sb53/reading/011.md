I'd guessed that in unit 7.
- The "existing law" here is the one I guessed for unit 7 (the Department of Technology and the high-risk automated decision system inventory). It just came under the Government Code item, not first.

## Wandering

- The baseline is chosen to show where CalCompute will live: the Department of Technology, under the Government Operations Agency. The ADS inventory (AB 302, 2023, I think) is the department's existing AI responsibility. So **CalCompute is framed as a state-IT capacity project**, not a safety measure. Safety and public compute sit side by side in one bill. One reading: the bill pairs a regulatory burden on large developers with a public investment that benefits researchers and startups. That's a political package, "safety plus innovation". A source model of SB 53 should record that the bill holds two theories at once: transparency for the frontier, and public compute for the ecosystem.
- "High-risk automated decision systems" is another neighbouring noun, from the EU-style "high-risk" vocabulary, about consequential decisions. It has nothing to do with frontier catastrophe. The lexicon will want to keep **"high-risk" (the decision-system sense)** apart from **"catastrophic risk" (SB 53's sense)**. A careless crosswalk could merge them because both say "risk".
