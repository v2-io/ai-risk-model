# Unit 202 · L285 · p.11

> (e) (1) A large frontier developer shall provide a reasonable internal process through which a covered employee may anonymously disclose information … if the covered employee believes in good faith that the information indicates that the large frontier developer's activities present a specific and substantial danger … or that the large frontier developer violated Chapter 25.1 …, including a monthly update to the person who made the disclosure regarding the status of the large frontier developer's investigation … and the actions taken …

## What differed

My recall was exact, including the "monthly update" the digest left out (u15). The digest's "a certain internal process" is "a **reasonable** internal process" in the statute. That's a third case of the digest dropping a qualifier, after "comply with" (u67) and the large-only penalty (u143).

## Wandering

- **The two belief standards are now confirmed in the statute** (they were only digest-level in u14–15). The anti-retaliation rule (a) uses "reasonable cause to believe … discloses", and the internal channel (e) uses "believes in good faith … indicates". The digest was accurate on this. The pose/present, discloses/indicates and reasonable/good-faith differences are all in the operative text. So the internal channel is looser on every axis, by design.
- **How do you update an anonymous reporter monthly?** The process has to keep a return channel open (a case number or an anonymous mailbox). "Reasonable internal process" leaves it to the developer. This is a real design detail that serious whistleblower systems handle (EthicsPoint, Navex). The statute's requirement is effectively that the developer use such a system.
- "**Investigation of the disclosure and the actions taken**": the duty to investigate is implied, not stated. A monthly status update presupposes an investigation. "No investigation; no action" would be an honest update that complies on its face, and it would be evidence for a later claim. The *reasonableness* of the process is the hook.
- **Tier: large frontier developers only**, as the digest said.
