# Unit 141 · L211 · p.8

> (2) The Attorney General shall not include information in a report pursuant to this subdivision that would compromise the trade secrets or cybersecurity of a frontier developer, confidentiality of a covered employee, public safety, or the national security of the United States or that would be prohibited by any federal or state law.

## What differed

As predicted, including "confidentiality of a covered employee".

## Wandering

- **The confidentiality value set gains a member here:** covered employees' confidentiality. The set is trade secrets, cybersecurity, public safety and national security, plus whistleblower confidentiality in the whistleblower context. It's a consistent and context-sensitive design. It's also an edit made to a copied provision (u140): the drafters adapted the OES template rather than pasting it blindly.
- Small-n anonymity again (u117). With perhaps a handful of whistleblower reports a year across about five large labs, "anonymized and aggregated" may still point to a specific person. The AG must withhold whatever would compromise confidentiality, which in small years may mean saying almost nothing.
