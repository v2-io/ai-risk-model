# Unit 140 · L210 · p.8

> (d) (1) Beginning January 1, 2027, and annually thereafter, the Attorney General shall produce a report with anonymized and aggregated information about reports from covered employees made pursuant to Chapter 5.1 … of the Labor Code that have been reviewed by the Attorney General since the preceding report.

## What differed

Not enforcement. It's an AG annual report on whistleblower reports, and it sits **inside §22757.14**, the Department of Technology's definitions-review section.

## Wandering

- **An AG duty in a section about the Department of Technology's review.** §22757.14(a)–(c) is the Department of Technology's definitions review; (d) gives the AG an annual whistleblower-report summary. The two don't belong together by subject. It's a structural seam, probably from amendment: (d) was likely added late and placed in the section about annual reports. A retrieval or tagging task that assumes "§22757.14 = definitions review" would miss this. **For section tagging: §22757.14 has two topics, (a)–(c) definitions review by the Department of Technology, and (d) the AG's whistleblower aggregate.**
- **The AG receives and reviews covered-employee reports.** Unit 114's guess is confirmed: the AG is the hub for whistleblower reports, as OES is for incidents. The two aggregate reports mirror each other in form (anonymized, aggregated, annual from 2027, "reviewed by … since the preceding report"). The phrasing is copied almost word for word from §22757.13(g)(1). **An internal copy edge:** the AG provision is modelled on the OES one.
- **Three annual reports from January 1, 2027:** the OES incident aggregate, the Department of Technology's definition recommendations, and the AG's whistleblower aggregate. (Plus CalCompute, if funded.) January 1, 2027 is the bill's main date for producing information.
- "That have been reviewed by the Attorney General" makes review discretionary in effect: only reviewed reports count, as with OES.
