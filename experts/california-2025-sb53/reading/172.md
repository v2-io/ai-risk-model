# Unit 172 · L248 · p.10

> (i) The consortium shall be dissolved upon submission of the report required by paragraph (1) of subdivision (f) to the Legislature.

## What differed

My recall was exact.

## Wandering

- "**Upon submission**": if the report is never submitted, the consortium never dissolves. A late report keeps it alive. There's no hard sunset date, only a deliverable-triggered one. A small oddity. Most sunsets are dated.
- So the consortium has one product, one deadline (soft, given the appropriation contingency), and dissolves when it delivers. It's an ad hoc study body.
