# Unit 215 · L302 · p.12

> (2) The remedies provided by this section are cumulative to each other and the remedies or penalties available under all other laws of this state.

## What differed

This is the cumulative-remedies clause I'd predicted at u212, arriving here.

## Wandering

- Cumulative remedies mean a covered employee can pursue §1107.1, §1102.5 and common-law wrongful termination (Tameny claims) together. They stack.
- **The TFAIA has no equivalent clause.** It says nothing about whether its remedies are cumulative, and its AG-only penalty clause (u144) is phrased narrowly. So whether a §17200 unfair-competition action can rest on a TFAIA violation stays open (u143–144). The Labor Code chapter is explicit about cumulation; the B&P chapter is silent. That's another asymmetry.
