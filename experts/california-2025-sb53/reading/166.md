## What differed

Fourteen members, as recalled. "**Consistent with state constitutional law**" is new to me.

## Wandering

- Why "consistent with state constitutional law"? Probably separation of powers in appointments. California's courts have limited legislative appointments to executive bodies (the Legislature appointing members of an executive-branch body raises separation-of-powers problems), so the qualifier shields the appointment scheme. It may also be about UC's constitutional autonomy (u151). Either way, the drafters expected a constitutional challenge to the composition and wrote in a saving phrase. The structure itself signals there may be a separation-of-powers issue.
