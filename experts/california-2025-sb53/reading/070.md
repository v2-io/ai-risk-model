## Wandering

- **"The potential for catastrophic risks"** is the doubled modality again ("potential for" a "risk"), now in operative text, not only in findings or the digest. So my unit-8 question gets its answer: "potential for catastrophic risk" isn't a digest slip. It's the statute's own idiom, and it means what "catastrophic risk" means. The defined term is already a risk. "Potential for" adds no layer. **Lexicon note: in SB 53, "potential for catastrophic risk" ≡ "catastrophic risk", by idiom.** A careful extractor shouldn't create a separate concept for it.
- "Based on the results of assessments" links mitigations to assessment results: the if-then structure of RSPs ("if capability X, then safeguard Y"). The framework must describe how mitigations follow from results. It needn't say mitigations must *succeed*. There's no adequacy standard, only "applying mitigations to address". Maybe item (4) covers adequacy.
- "Pursuant to paragraph (2)" is a clean internal cross-reference. Good drafting.
- The list so far reads like a framework template: (1) standards, (2) thresholds, (3) mitigations. The pattern matches the Frontier Model Forum's framework-components guidance, or the Seoul commitments' list ("outcome 1–5"), or METR's "common elements of frontier AI safety policies" (2024–25). Probably METR's common-elements analysis or the Seoul Frontier AI Safety Commitments are a lineage source for this list. I can't confirm from the text. A question for the corpus, later.
