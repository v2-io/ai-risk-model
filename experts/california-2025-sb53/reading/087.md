## Wandering

- **The base transparency report has no risk content at all.** For every frontier developer, (c)(1) requires seven items:
  1. website;
  2. contact mechanism;
  3. release date;
  4. languages;
  5. output modalities;
  6. intended uses;
  7. generally applicable use restrictions (terms of service, acceptable use policy).

So **a small frontier developer's transparency report is a minimal model card with no safety information.** Findings (f) ("access to information regarding … frontier AI capabilities") and (k) ("alert policymakers and the public to serious risks") aren't served by (c)(1) at all. Risk content must come in a (c)(2) for large developers, if my structure guess is right.
