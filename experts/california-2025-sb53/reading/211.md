# Unit 211 · L298 · p.12

> (4) Appropriate injunctive relief shall be issued on a showing that reasonable cause exists to believe a violation has occurred.

## What differed

As predicted.

## Wandering

- **A low bar for an injunction.** "Reasonable cause exists to believe a violation has occurred" is far easier to meet than the usual preliminary-injunction test (likelihood of success, irreparable harm, balance of hardships). And "**shall** be issued", so it's mandatory once reasonable cause is shown. It's strongly pro-employee. That's the 1102.61 template's design, carried over.
- Another epistemic standard: "reasonable cause exists to believe" (the court's view of the evidence). Compare (a)'s "reasonable cause to believe" (the employee's). It's the same phrase used for two different believers. The table in u206 should note that it applies to both.
