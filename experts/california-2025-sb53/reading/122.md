## Wandering

- **A non-condition in a list of conditions.** (h) introduced "all of the following conditions", and (1)(B) is a *negative clarification*: it says what isn't needed. That's the type inconsistency again (u48, u53), now a third instance. Enumerations in SB 53 keep mixing kinds: here "conditions" contains a non-condition. An extractor that turns each item into a requirement will get this one backwards.
- **The effect:** a developer reporting to a *federal* agency under an equivalent standard needn't report to California at all, if designated. California gives up the data flow. So if federal equivalence is designated, **OES may receive nothing**, and the annual report (u117) would be empty, unless (i) requires a copy. Watch (i).
