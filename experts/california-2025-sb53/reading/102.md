## Wandering

- "**A mechanism … to report a critical safety incident that includes all of the following**": what includes the following, the mechanism or the report? Grammatically the nearest antecedent is "a critical safety incident", which is nonsense (incidents don't include fields). Next is the mechanism. Intended: the mechanism collects these fields. So OES must build an intake that asks for specific information. That's the **standardization** finding (l) promised ("standardized … reporting"): a fixed field list for incident reports, at least.
- **Who reports:** "a frontier developer or a member of the public". Not "large", so all frontier developers. And any member of the public. Not covered employees specifically: they're members of the public too, or they use the whistleblower channel. A member of the public could report an incident with a lab's model directly to OES. But members of the public can't know most incident kinds: weight exfiltration, internal deception. They'd mostly report (d)(2) materialized-catastrophe harms or (d)(3) loss-of-control injuries they witness. A public-report channel for incidents defined mostly by internal lab events is an odd fit.
