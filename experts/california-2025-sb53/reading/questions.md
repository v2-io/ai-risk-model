# Questions for the SB 53 expert

*Kept while reading. **Likely**: questions people will probably ask. **Should**: questions they should ask but might not think to. Each notes the unit where it came up; answers are added only once the text settles them.*

## Likely

- Which version of SB 53 is this? *(u1–5: chaptered, Stats. 2025 ch. 138, approved and filed 2025-09-29.)*
- When does it take effect? Keep apart the published date (2025-09-29 9pm), the approved and filed date (2025-09-29), the default operative date (2026-01-01; no urgency clause), and any operative dates inside the text. CalCompute is operative only on appropriation. *(u2, u12, u19)*
- Who enforces it? *(u10: the AG, by civil penalty, for the TFAIA. u15: the whistleblower part gives employees civil actions with attorney's fees.)*
- Does SB 53 create CalCompute? *(u12: no, not quite. On appropriation, a consortium develops a framework for one, reports by 2027-01-01, and dissolves.)*
- What counts as a catastrophic risk / frontier developer / frontier model? *(Not in the digest; from the text.)*

## Should (but might not)

- **Is this passage the digest or the enacted text?** The digest (L30–60) isn't law, has no definitions, contains at least one wrong word ("internet use", u9), and is keyword-dense, so it may rank high for queries it can't answer. *(u6, u18)*
- **Does SB 53 require developers to comply with their own frameworks?** I mustn't answer from memory: my recall said "write, implement, comply with, and … publish", and the digest omits "comply with". *(u8)* The answer also sets how broad whistleblower prong (b) is. *(u14)*
- **Which tier does a given obligation attach to:** frontier developer or large frontier developer? *(u8, u15)*
- **Which belief standard applies?** It differs by provision, at least in the digest: "reasonable cause to believe" for anti-retaliation, "believes in good faith" for the internal channel. *(u14–15)*
- **Can a whistleblower go public?** Per the digest, the protected recipients are the AG, a federal authority, a supervisor and an authorised co-worker. Not OES, not the press. *(u14)*
- **What does the public actually get to see?** Published frameworks (and transparency reports?). Incident reports, internal-use assessments and whistleblower reports are exempt from the PRA. Is anything aggregated and published? *(u9)*
- **What does preemption cover?** Local only; adopted on or after 2025-01-01; specific to frontier developers; their management of catastrophic risk. Not state law, not other topics. *(u16)*
- **"Safe" in CalCompute and "safety" in "critical safety incident" are probably different concepts.** *(u12)*
- **"High-risk automated decision systems" (Government Code baseline) are unrelated to SB 53's "catastrophic risk".** *(u11)*
