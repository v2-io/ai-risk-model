# 018 · L330–338 · the disclaimer

**Predicted:** not the views of institutions or governments; independent editorial control. **Got:** close, with two surprises.
- "This Report is a synthesis of the existing research on the capabilities and risks of **advanced AI**". In the blurb (002) the object was "**general-purpose AI** systems". Two names for the object within a page. Is "advanced AI" a looser synonym here, or a broader object? The definitions in 1.1 should settle what the report means. Hold the drift as a question; it isn't an error yet.
- "does not necessarily represent the views of **the Chair**, any particular individual in the writing or advisory groups, nor any of the governments" … yet "**The Chair of the Report has ultimate responsibility for it**". Responsibility without endorsement: a consensus-synthesis posture. The document's claims are claims about the *literature* and the state of evidence, not anyone's beliefs. **For assertion-recording in this project, that matters.** An IASR sentence like "X is likely" has the speech-act force of "the evidence surveyed supports X is likely", attributable to the Report, under the Chair's responsibility, not to Bengio as a person or to any government. Different from a statute (SB 53) or a company's own claim (the Risk Report).
- "**Research series number: DSIT 2026/001**": it's formally a UK government research publication. With "Crown owned", the UK is the publisher of record even though the panel is international.

**Next, I predict:** Forewords. Bengio's own, perhaps one from the Indian hosts (MeitY) or the UK minister. Bengio will stress the pace of change, the growing evidence, and the importance of a shared scientific basis across countries.
