# 079 · L926–934 · p.39 · OECD Scenario 4: Progress accelerates; the close of the scenarios

**Predicted:** AI R&D automation, superhuman; an analogue such as fission. **Got:** "**human-level remote workers**"; analogue: DNA sequencing's "superexponential" improvement 2000–2020 through new paradigms.
- **The 2030 state:** "autonomy and cognitive capability **match or surpass humans in cognitive tasks**"; "autonomously work towards **broad strategic goals that they can reflect upon and revise** if circumstances change"; "seamlessly learn … during deployment"; robots in "dynamic real-world environments", though physical tasks still "largely lag humans' … unless the system was developed specifically".
- **"Broad strategic goals that they can reflect upon and revise"** is the most LoC-relevant phrase in the capability chapter: goal revision by the system itself. It's stated neutrally as a capability. The loss-of-control section (2.2.2) said "Current systems lack the capabilities to pose such risks" (028). Scenario 4 describes systems that plausibly would. **So the report's capability scenarios set up the conditions under which its LoC risk becomes live, by 2030, in a scenario it calls "plausible".** It doesn't (yet) connect them. Watch 2.2.2.
- **Pathway:** exponential gains in the existing paradigms, plus "significant algorithmic breakthroughs" and "**increasingly substantial contributions from AI coding assistants to the development of AI**". AI R&D automation, as I predicted, phrased modestly ("coding assistants").
- **The ceiling of the scenario set is human-level-or-above, not superintelligence.** Scenario 4's top says "as or more capable than humans across most or all capability dimensions". The scenario set doesn't go to vastly superhuman systems by 2030. That's a framing choice by the OECD. **Should-ask: "Does IASR consider superintelligence by 2030?"** The scenarios top out at "match or surpass humans in cognitive tasks"; the 073 FRI question ("decades of progress could happen in years") is the nearest thing to explosive acceleration.
- **The closing sentence:** "by 2030, AI progress could plausibly range from **stagnation** to rapid improvement to levels that **exceed human cognitive performance**." "Stagnation" here vs "stalls" / "plateaus" in Scenario 1. The same thing.
- **No probabilities over scenarios.** "All plausible". FRI gives probabilities for specific questions; the OECD gives scenario classes without weights. The pairing is deliberate: forecasts for quantities, scenarios for structure.
