- **Figure 2.1: the OECD AI Incidents and Hazards Monitor** ("content generation" events rising since 2021). **The first appearance of the word "hazards" in the report, inside a proper name.** The OECD distinguishes an AI *incident* (harm occurred) from an AI *hazard* (an event that could plausibly lead to an incident). IASR cites the database but, so far, hasn't defined "hazard" itself. **For the project's "hazard in eight senses" question:** IASR may never define "hazard". If so, its usage is only the OECD's, by citation. Watch the Glossary.
- "While the number of reported incidents is rising … systematic data on the frequency or severity … is limited." Reported incidents ≠ incidence: reporting growth can reflect attention, not frequency. The report doesn't say that explicitly, but "systematic data … limited" implies it.
