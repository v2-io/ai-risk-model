# 026 · L456–458 · p.12 · ES 2 heading and its one-line taxonomy

"General-purpose AI risks fall into three categories: malicious use, malfunctions, and systemic risks." A flat assertion that the three are exhaustive ("fall into"), without "we organise them as". It's presented as a fact about the risks rather than as the report's own organising scheme. The project's CLAUDE.md principle holds: this is a taxonomy the source asserts. Its basis of division seems mixed: malicious use is defined by *intent of a user*, malfunction by *the system behaving other than intended*, systemic by *scale or diffusion*. These aren't one basis, so a risk can belong to two (e.g. manipulation that's both malicious use and systemic). The report must deal with overlaps somewhere; watch for a statement on them.

**Next, I predict:** a paragraph for each category, malicious use first (deepfakes, scams, cyber, bio), with "real-world evidence" foregrounded.
