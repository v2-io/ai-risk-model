# 033 · L518–528 · p.14 · "Changes since the 2025 Report"

**Predicted:** scope narrowing, scenarios, Key Updates, new formats. **Got:** the new formats, two definitions, and a division of labour between FRI and the OECD. The scope narrowing *isn't* repeated here, interestingly, and the Key Updates aren't mentioned at all.

- **"'reasoning systems'"**, defined in passing: systems using "techniques that allow systems to use more computing power to generate intermediate steps before giving a final answer". The same mechanism as "inference-time scaling" (025). So IASR's family is: inference-time scaling (the technique) → reasoning systems (the systems that use it). Note "systems", not "models". The 2025 report's careful model/system distinction (if I remember it right) blurs here.
- **"AI agents – systems that can act in the world with limited human oversight"** is a key lexicon item. The defining features: (1) *acting in the world* and (2) *limited human oversight*. Not tool use, not goals, not planning. Oversight is the defining variable, so "agent" here is defined *relationally* (relative to human oversight), not by internal architecture. That fits the report's risk lens: agents matter because oversight is reduced (028: "harder for humans to intervene"). Compare other sources: the EU Code, Anthropic's report. The Glossary may define it differently; check.
- "increasingly capable and reliable, though they remain **prone to basic errors**": consistent with jaggedness.
- **Bio, the third phrasing:** "unable to **confidently** rule out the possibility that these models could assist novices with **weapon development**". "Confidently" is added, and the object is now "weapon development" generally rather than "biological weapons". Slight drift each time. The canonical one will be in 2.1.4.
- **The FRI/OECD split:** "capability **forecasts** developed with the **Forecasting Research Institute** and **scenarios** developed with the **OECD**". Forecasts (probabilistic, presumably elicited) from FRI; scenarios (qualitative alternatives) from the OECD. "Developed with" means co-developed with the report team, so not purely external. Attribution for a fork: "FRI forecasts, commissioned or co-developed for IASR 2026".
- **New per-section structure:** "Each section includes **updates since the last Report**, **key challenges for policymakers**, and **evidence gaps** to guide future research." So every section has three labelled sub-parts. **This is very useful for tagging and search.** A fork asked "what does IASR see as the evidence gaps on cyber?" can go to that section's "evidence gaps" sub-part. I expect them as labelled blocks.
