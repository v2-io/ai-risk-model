**The 77% is AIxCC after all, and the summaries misstate it.**
- Body: "Competitors in the final phase of the **DARPA AI Cyber Challenge (AIxCC)** … One AI system autonomously identified **77% of the vulnerabilities introduced by the competition organisers**, as well as other, unintentional vulnerabilities."
- ES (027): "In one competition, an AI agent identified 77% of the vulnerabilities **present in real software**."

- **What's wrong with the summaries:**
  1. The vulnerabilities were *introduced by the organisers* (synthetic, injected into real codebases), not naturally "present in real software". (The system *also* found some unintentional, real ones; that part is genuine.)
  2. AIxCC's final had AI systems only, a handful of finalist teams, not "over 400 (mostly human) teams". The "top 5% of over 400 teams" placement must come from a *different* result (a CTF with human teams; cf. 117's picoCTF "top 3%"). **The Key information seems to merge two findings into one sentence.**

The Key information's "top 5% of over 400 (mostly human) teams" doesn't fit AIxCC and appears to be a conflation.
