# 098 · L1080–1084 · p.49 · §2.1.1 Mitigations

**Predicted:** challenges for policymakers. **Got:** a "**Mitigations**" sub-part. So risk sections have Key information / body / Updates / Evidence gaps / **Mitigations** / (Challenges?). Another tag for the structure.

- **Detection: "mixed effectiveness"**. Warning labels have a "modest impact": accuracy at identifying AI videos went "**from 10.7% to 21.6%**" with labels. Note those are low baselines: people identified only about 1 in 10 AI videos unaided. Detector tools: "effectiveness remains limited".
- **Prevention:** "gating access to AI models – for example, limiting access to **vetted users**" (structured access), and safeguards (classifiers, filters, rules). "in the case of **open-weight models**, malicious actors can bypass these measures". **"Filtering sexual content from models' training data is also emerging as an effective method"**. A positive mitigation finding, rare in this section, and linked to 042's data-curation-for-safety.
- **"Watermarking"**, defined: "embedding a machine-readable digital signature into the content during creation, allowing for automated traceable verification of its origin and authenticity". Limits: removable, detectors can be deceived, weaker for open weights.
- **Provenance for authentic media:** "recording devices can be required to embed unique digital signatures" (C2PA-like; "can be required" is a policy option phrased neutrally).
- **Output logs:** compare new content against logs of generated outputs. Scalability, evasion, **privacy** concerns.
- **Defence in depth, again:** "a combination of these mitigations within a broader ecosystem of standards and policies can compensate for their respective limitations". The ES's headline (layering) appears at section level. So "layering" is the report's recurring answer in every section, and probably a core thesis.

Wandering: privacy, excluded as a *risk* (022), reappears as a *cost of mitigation* (logging). The narrowed scope keeps re-admitting its excluded topics in other roles: bias as capability limitation (059), environment and market concentration as bottlenecks (086), privacy as mitigation cost (here). **That's a recurring pattern worth one note in my outline:** IASR's excluded risks remain present as constraints, limitations and trade-offs.
