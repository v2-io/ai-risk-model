- **"Compute"** in quotes: "Computational resources … include specialised computer chips as well as the software and infrastructure needed to run them". An asterisked footnote gives the other two senses: the number of calculations (FLOP/s) "or specifically the hardware (such as graphics processing units)". **Three senses of "compute", declared by the source**: resources (chips + software + infrastructure), a measurement (FLOP/s; strictly, a *rate*), and hardware. Lexicon: `iasr:compute` is polysemous by the source's own admission. (Note "floating-point operations per second" is the rate; training compute is usually total FLOP. A small conflation, or deliberate.)
