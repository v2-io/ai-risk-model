## Unit 112 (L756-760, p.49 "47"): Appendix, Uncertainty; CORRECTION to unit 25
Text: success per task averaged over 10 repeats; "the standard errors for evaluations are calculated using the standard error of the mean formula (SEM = std/sqrt(n)) applied to task-level success rates": per model, success rate for each task (e.g. 0.3 for 3/10), then "the standard deviation of these task success rates divided by the square root of the number of tasks. Correction: in unit 25 I wrote that the error bars "capture sampling noise from the model's stochasticity, not uncertainty over which tasks were chosen".
