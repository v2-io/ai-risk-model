# Unit 130 — §3.2 note: why AI R&D is the focus, L1697–1703, p.96

Three reasons: (1) AI R&D plays to current AI strengths, so acceleration shows there first; (2) measurable earlier and with more fidelity, because Anthropic uses its models extensively for AI R&D; (3) recursive self-improvement — dramatic AI R&D acceleration "could lead in short order to much *more* powerful AI systems", raising almost all other threat models. Other domains (§3.6): "current AIs are very far from being capable of full automation or transformative acceleration in these domains."

Differed: "recursive self-improvement" named explicitly as the mechanism of concern. And a strong, flat claim on other domains ("very far").

Wandering: reason (2) is a measurement-convenience rationale: Anthropic is its own best observatory, because it is the heaviest user of its models for AI R&D. That's a kind of insider epistemic advantage — and an insider bias (it measures acceleration of *its own* R&D, which is also the quantity its commercial strategy depends on). For the project: the source measures the threat on itself. Noted neutrally.

"Very far" on other domains, against expert interviews whose external experts used sub-Mythos models (unit 128). An expert should attach that caveat to the "very far" claim.
