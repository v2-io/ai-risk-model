- **Footnotes 6–9 (for the CB-2 threshold in unit 9) arrived here,** displaced by the conversion:

  - fn 7, on "well-resourced team": "For example, a program with millions of dollars available and support from recent PhD graduates from top-tier international programs. Same tier, two words.
  - fn 8, on "would otherwise require": "E.g., in a world with access only to the best AI models as of 2023." — **the counterfactual baseline is 2023-era AI**.
  - fn 9, on "a small number of world-leading specialists": "E.g., hundreds."

## Wandering

Footnote 8 matters for the project's catalogue of counterfactual baselines. This report alone now has at least two: (i) "marginal risk" relative to *other developers' current systems* (§1.1); (ii) CB-2 substitution relative to *a world with only 2023's best models*. The 2023 baseline means uplift already provided by 2024–2026 models from any lab counts toward the threshold — more inclusive than "relative to other labs". They answer different questions (threshold-crossing vs marginal contribution), and a careless translation will merge them.

"Hundreds" of world-leading specialists — the scarcity being substituted for is hundreds of people worldwide.

I was wrong in unit 9 in exactly the way experiential reading exposes: the conversion displaced the footnotes, and I reasoned from their absence. A fork quoting the CB-2 threshold must carry footnotes 6–9 with it.

Coverage date + 30-day rule: a risk report is a snapshot with an as-of date, like a balance-sheet date. The project could treat "coverage date" as a standard field for periodic assessments.
