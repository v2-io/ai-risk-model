## Wandering

**Model 2 at 62.8% against an 85% bar.** That's the most concrete measure of distance to criterion 1 in the report: ~22 points for the most capable internal model, with a harness the authors think could gain "significantly". Mythos Preview → Model 2 rose ~8 points. An expert asked "how close is Anthropic to its AI R&D threshold?" should give these figures, the 85% bar, and the "significant further gains" caveat — and note that the bar is the authors' judgement of what a substituting model would score, not a measured human baseline.

**Mythos 5 (50.3%) below Mythos Preview (54.8%)** — even though the set was filtered *against* Mythos Preview (problems it failed at least once in three tries). A filter biased against Mythos Preview still leaves it ahead of Mythos 5. That joins the stealth/secret-keeping pattern (units 27–29): Mythos Preview is stronger than Mythos 5 on several capability measures. So "Mythos 5" isn't simply a stronger successor on every axis; possibly a more safeguarded/aligned/cheaper variant. The report doesn't explain. Question for an expert: *why does Mythos 5 trail Mythos Preview on CoBench, SHADE-Arena stealth, and secret-keeping-in-thinking?*

Filtering by Mythos Preview's failures makes scores across models not directly comparable to an unfiltered distribution, and depresses Mythos Preview's score in particular (selection on its own failures). The report states the filter; a fork must keep it attached.

Model-graded with a rubric comparing to the true root cause: the grader-model susceptibility seen in unit 41 (confident wrong answers accepted) is a potential inflator. The report says rubrics were validated manually.
