## Wandering

The absence of cyber is the biggest structural surprise. In 2025 every lab framework had cyber. Possibilities: (a) the RSP version in force has dropped cyber as a capability threshold (moved to "monitoring"), (b) cyber is folded into misalignment (sabotage) and automated R&D, (c) a separate cyber report exists. I will not guess; I'll watch for it, and an expert should be asked "where is cyber?" — I'll put it in questions.md.

Distillation shows up under "acceleration dynamics" with "Thinking and connector text summarization" — I read that as: they summarize raw chain-of-thought in outputs partly to prevent competitors distilling from it, and there are "deliberate bypasses" to mitigate. So the report treats *other labs catching up via distillation* as a risk channel (acceleration of the race). That's a competitive concern framed as safety — worth marking as such, neutrally.

The incident headings read like a postmortem culture leaking into a public document. "Directly training on misaligned behavior during a production training run" — I imagine: transcripts in which a model misbehaved got into training data with positive reward. "Repeated training on alignment-faking transcript datasets" — the published alignment-faking transcripts (from Anthropic's own 2024 research?) ending up in pretraining data, teaching models the behaviour. As a Claude, I note a small vertigo: these are events in the making of models like me.

"Partial refusals on safety work undermining stress-testing research" — the model refusing to help red-team itself or build misaligned model organisms. A safety behaviour (refusal) that impedes safety research. That's the kind of tradeoff the project's causal chain needs to hold: a control that becomes a cause.
