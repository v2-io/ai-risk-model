# Unit 195 — §4.5.5.3.1 Helpful-only models, L2505–2523, p.142

- Helpful-only variants (no refusals) for dangerous-capability evals and some internal red-teaming (fn 70).
- **Internal access**: policy restricts to a very limited set of staff with manual approval, but **"technical controls to prevent such access in violation of our policies are fairly limited, and we expect that technically sophisticated employees could still obtain such access via higher-friction routes"**; more controls planned "later this year".
- **External**: Helpful-Only Model Access Policy — only external AI safety testing partners meeting security requirements (fn 71); **six organisations, 48 users** as of the coverage date (fn 72); plus limited-duration access for individual external safety researchers. Several redactions (internal usage, public safety, organisation details).

Differed: a frank admission that the *insider* control on helpful-only models is mostly policy, not technical — relevant to the §3.10 insider standard ("even malicious employees … with maximal access will not be significantly enabled") which, by this, Anthropic does not currently meet for helpful-only models. The report doesn't draw that comparison; an expert should.

Wandering: helpful-only models are the highest-hazard artefacts in the CB picture (capable and unrefusing), and the internal control is "policy plus friction". The CB-2 risk from an insider with a Mythos-class helpful-only model would be the scenario where safeguards are absent by design. The threat models address external actors; the insider route for CB is thinly covered. For the project: an acknowledged control gap with a stated remediation timeline (later in 2026).
