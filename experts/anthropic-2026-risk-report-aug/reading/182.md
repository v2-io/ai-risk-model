# Unit 182 — §4.5.3.2.1 Boundary-point jailbreaking (status), L2388–2390, p.136

A UK AISI-developed automated methodology against black-box classifiers; concerning because its jailbreaks cause little capability degradation (unlike most universal jailbreaks). High-level method public; no known public implementation effective against Anthropic's safeguards at any robustness level.

Differed: this is the route referenced at Level 2 ("could still be used, with difficulty"). Its significance for the report's argument: it undercuts the unit-165 mitigating factor that "many jailbreaks degrade capabilities".

Wandering: a government evaluator developing an attack method that is publicly described at a high level — the standard security-research dynamic (publish method, withhold working implementations). For the project: AISI as a creator of attack techniques, not only a tester.
