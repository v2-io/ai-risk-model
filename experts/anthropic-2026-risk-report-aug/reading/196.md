# Unit 196 — §4.5.5.3.2 Claude Gov, L2525–2531, p.143

- Claude Gov: post-trained for national-security uses (fewer refusals on national-security-relevant requests), on AWS Secret and Top Secret Cloud.
- **Gov Sonnet 4.5 runs without CB safeguards**; only for users with US Secret/Top Secret clearances on accredited isolated networks from approved locations; served to as many authorised users as capacity allows ("millions of individuals have U.S. Secret or Top Secret clearances").
- All other Claude Gov deployments carry the same blocking CB classifiers as the corresponding commercial model; exceptions case by case; CB capabilities essentially identical to the commercial models.

Differed: the identity-vetting logic of exemptions, scaled up to a government clearance population — potentially millions of eligible users for an unsafeguarded Sonnet 4.5. Vetting is outsourced to the US clearance system.

Wandering: for the project, three regimes now: commercial (content blocking), exempt orgs (Anthropic vetting + monitoring), Claude Gov (state vetting + accredited networks, no CB blocking for Gov Sonnet 4.5). The report frames the clearance population size plainly. Sonnet 4.5 is a Level 2 model commercially (unit 174) and well below Mythos-class, which is the implicit risk argument. Insider threat within a cleared population is the residual (cf. the insider standard of §3.10). Pathway 8 (§2: undermining government decisions) is the misalignment-side counterpart for government deployment.
