# Unit 188 — §4.5.3.2.3 Coverage gap (status), L2439–2445, p.139

(Status-only.) A gap in classifiers' training-data coverage found internally, later reported similarly by UK AISI; details redacted until mitigations are on all models; new expanded-coverage classifiers rolling out; remaining known bypasses limited to generic low-uplift tasks also available on much weaker models.

Differed: redaction justified by *deployment status* ("not yet deployed our mitigations on all models") — a time-bound redaction, implying the details might be published once fixed. Grounds for redaction so far: IP, commercial sensitivity, public safety, infohazard/training-data leakage, and now "not yet patched everywhere".

Wandering: internal discovery preceded AISI's — the report notes order of discovery, which matters for crediting independent corroboration: AISI's finding is independent confirmation, not the original discovery.
