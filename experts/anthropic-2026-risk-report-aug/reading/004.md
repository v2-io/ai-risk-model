# Unit 4 — §1 Introduction (opening), L202–220, p.7

## What differed

- **RSP version 3.4.** I guessed "≥2.x, perhaps 3.0". So the RSP has gone through 3.0→3.4 by Aug 2026 (and §1.3 lists changes since the last report — so 3.x point releases are frequent).
- **Cadence: "every 3–6 months"**, not semiannual.
- **Scope claim: "a risk assessment of Anthropic's activities as a whole"**, including models run only internally — not a per-model document. The unit of assessment is *the company's activities*, and the object of judgment is "the degree to which Anthropic's AI systems pose catastrophic risk in several categories". These are two different scopes (activities vs systems) stated two paragraphs apart; the report blurs them. Worth holding: is the final judgment about systems or about the company?
- **Risk = model properties × mitigation properties** ("considering the whole picture of both AI model properties and risk mitigations"). This is an explicit statement of the report's model of risk: risk is assessed *net of* controls. The system cards assess capability; the Risk Report assesses residual risk.
- The relation to regulation: "Where regulatory requirements exceed or differ from what is covered here, we will address them through separate documents." So this is *not* Anthropic's SB 53 "frontier AI framework" or its EU Code Safety and Security Model Report — or at least claims not to be bound as one. That's a significant scoping sentence for this project, which compares it against SB 53 and the EU Code.

## Wandering

"A small number of particularly salient catastrophic risks, for reasons discussed below" — the selection rule is promised; probably §6.1 threat model criteria. This is where cyber's absence might be justified.

"Catastrophic risk" is used without definition so far. The project's corpus has SB 53's (>50 deaths or >$1B) and OpenAI's ("thousands… hundreds of billions"). Prediction: Anthropic will define it loosely or by reference to the RSP, not numerically. If they give a number, it will be a key data point.

Footnote 1 ("We also conduct periodic analysis to evaluate other emerging risks.") — content not shown in this unit; the footnote text will come later in the stream probably.

The Usage Policy and the Alignment/Societal Impacts/Safeguards/Security teams are named as covering "other concerns" — a division of labour: catastrophic risk here, everything else elsewhere. The report's bounded context is narrow by design.
