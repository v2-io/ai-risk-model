Differed: a *retroactive* revision of the previous report's rating — an assessment of a past period updated by later-discovered facts. And "not negligible" is new wording beyond the summary table's "Low".

Wandering: the retroactive revision is the right epistemic move and rare. For the project: an assertion about a past state, by the same source, superseding its own earlier assertion — a "revises" relation between two Risk Reports. An expert asked "what did Anthropic think CB-1 risk was in February?" must give both: "very low" (Feb report, at the time) and "low" (Aug report, in retrospect).

The four-point structure is a mini safety case per access route: weights, jailbreaks, other jailbreaks, exemption access. The weakest link named is access control — consistent with where failures occurred (vendors, contractors). Notice the argument is scoped to CB-1's modest-resource actors; the nation-state weight-theft gap (unit 200) is outside CB-1's actor set.
