# Unit 222 — §5.2.1 Introduction, L2852–2860, p.163

Examples of where safety/security posture fell short between the previous report and the coverage date. Unlike §4.5.8 (reasonably comprehensive for CB safeguards), this is **"only … a representative sample"**; **one case redacted** for public safety; a further redaction "to avoid uplifting attackers".

Differed: the scope word "representative sample" matters: the five listed failures are not the full set. An expert must never say "Anthropic had five safety process failures"; the report presents a sample, with one public-safety-redacted case among them (so the TOC's five headings plus at least one unlisted).

Wandering: who chose the sample, by what criterion, isn't said. A selected sample of one's own failures carries selection risk in both directions (representative vs most benign or most instructive). Mark as soft.
