## Wandering

The insufficiency admission is plain: if the threshold were crossed, current mitigations wouldn't keep risk low. So "Low" here rests entirely on the threshold not being met — the same structure as §2's reliance on Claim 1, but starker. An expert asked "what if automated R&D arrives?" should quote this sentence.

The acceleration criterion is assessed over the trajectory, not per model — a genuinely different unit of analysis from most risk frameworks (which assess a model). This report's object here is a *process* (Anthropic's rate of progress). It fits the report's scope claim "Anthropic's activities as a whole" (unit 4). For the project: a threat whose bearer is an organisation's R&D trajectory, not an artefact.

"Potential" dropped between §3.1 and the executive summary — the exec summary is the stronger statement. Small, but in the direction a careful reader should notice: the executive summary rounds up the evidence of acceleration here (as §2's summary rounded up "somewhat likely" in unit 83 the other way). I'll note it as a summary-vs-body drift.

External experts used *other companies'* models comparable to Opus 4.7/4.8 — below Mythos. So the other-domain assessment for external experts reflects sub-Mythos capability. The report says so; worth carrying: the other-domains evidence understates Mythos-class capability for external domains.
