- Scope: all publicly released models at least as capable as Claude Opus 4; mitigations vary (stronger for most capable/most widely deployed).
- **Not focused:** Opus 5, Model 1, Model 2 (no external usage as of the coverage date); their CB evals (limited for Models 1 and 2) "generally indicated performance comparable to or weaker than Claude Mythos 5 in all three cases."
- Models below Opus 4 (bottom three rows of Table 4.5.A): basic mitigations — Usage Policy (prohibits harmful CBRN use); harmlessness training; fine-tuning API protections (data filtering; automated harmlessness evals) — **"Some models with these fine-tuning protections removed are available to a very limited set of external users"** (§4.5.5.3.1); vulnerability reporting (in-product; usersafety@anthropic.com). Risk "very low due to their limited capabilities, and would remain so even if some of the above protections were disabled."
