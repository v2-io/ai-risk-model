## Wandering

The industry standard is phrased as a burden of argument: "should make a strong argument that…" — a safety-case standard for the whole industry, mirroring §2's argument form.

**RAND SL4** — a security level from RAND's weight-security framework (SL1–SL5), SL4 ≈ defending against state-level operations short of the most capable. A concrete external anchor. And the carve-out is notable: labs within "a credible industry-wide safety regime" needn't treat each other as threats to weights. So the recommended standard presupposes a regime that doesn't yet exist (unit's own phrasing "not bound by a credible industry-wide safety regime") — the RSP's industry recommendations are conditional on, and implicitly advocating for, such a regime. A policy position embedded in a standard; mark the interest.

**"up to and including the company's CEO"** — the insider-threat standard explicitly covers the CEO. That's a governance claim about restraining the most powerful insider, written into a recommendation. Whether Anthropic meets it isn't said yet (the next unit, probably "we fall short").
