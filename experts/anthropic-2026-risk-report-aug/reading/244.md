Each no-to-low risk, bounded (duration, surface, population, effect size), **identified internally** (not via external exploitation), remediated. (1) **Sonnet 4.6 launch preview** with unusually non-robust early classifiers for a small customer pool, short period; improved evals and earlier probe training. (2) **Helpful-only Mythos Preview access** for two external CBRN testing orgs continued **two weeks after launch** through failure to revoke; deprovisioning still manual; automatic expiry hoped for.

Differed: "identified through our own testing and monitoring rather than surfaced by external exploitation" — a claim for the minor list; contrasts with the major incidents (the Mythos Preview contractor access, found via an external report; the year-long vendor gap, found internally).

Wandering: manual credential revocation for helpful-only access is a small process gap with a recurring shape: access controls depending on someone remembering to act. The report names the fix (automatic expiry) as hoped-for, not done.
