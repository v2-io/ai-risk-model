# Unit 211 — §4.7 Looking forward (CB), L2706–2720, p.153

- Better evaluations and threat assessment; safeguards revisited.
- **"Our most capable models are relatively close to the CB-2 threshold … and we expect that subsequent Anthropic models will cross this threshold in the relatively near future."**
- RSP's planned mitigations for crossing CB-2 (quoted): *protections "at least as strong as our ASL-3 protections" applied to an expanded set of use cases covering the most likely vectors*; and *identify the most concerning pathways, create policy recommendations for early detection and response, share with policymakers*.
- Claimed status: first criterion already met for all models ≥ Opus 4 (§4.5.2.1), exceeded for Fable 5; second "also met", work ongoing; update in a future System Card or Risk Report if a model crosses CB-2.

Differed: two things. (1) A strong forecast: the *next* models are expected to cross CB-2 "in the relatively near future" — paralleling §3's "may cross this threshold in the coming year" for AI R&D. Both major thresholds are forecast to be crossed soon. (2) **"ASL-3" survives inside the RSP's own text**, even though this report retired the term (unit 174). So RSP v3.4 still says "ASL-3 protections" in its CB-2 mitigation clause, while the Risk Report says it no longer uses ASL terminology. A lexicon seam: the term is retired in the report but live in the governing policy text it quotes. An expert should flag it.

Wandering: the claim that the CB-2 mitigations are *already met* matters: if true, crossing CB-2 would require no new safeguards under the RSP beyond what's deployed — which is consistent with Fable 5's very broad coverage being, in effect, pre-emptive CB-2 safeguarding. It also means the RSP's CB-2 mitigation bar is defined relative to "ASL-3 protections", a level Anthropic reached in 2025 — so the bar is modest relative to the expected capability. The report doesn't characterise it that way; my reading, marked.

"Share with policymakers" — the second mitigation is a disclosure/advocacy act, not a technical control. For the project: a control of type *information to government*.
