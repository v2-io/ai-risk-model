## Wandering

The poisoning-teaches-evasion route is the sharpest version of my unit-58 worry: knowledge of monitoring is an attack surface. The report acknowledges it here as an *engineered* threat; the unintended version (public documents like this report describing monitoring tiers, entering pretraining) is the naturally-emerging twin, and isn't discussed as such. A data-poisoning attacker wouldn't need to write the monitoring details; Anthropic has published some of them.

Claim 8.2's motivation argument is unusual in a risk report: "unlikely motivated" — a claim about adversary intent. State actors seeking to compromise a frontier lab's models would be the natural adversary; "somewhat unlikely" to want *catastrophic* engineered misalignment, perhaps, but to want a backdoor (for espionage, sabotage of a rival nation's use)? The definition restricts to "significantly harm-inducing" in the pathway sense. Footnote 28 may qualify. Hold.

"As argued in prior risk reports" — plural again, with only one prior. Second occurrence; probably counts the Feb report and the "Alignment Risk Update" documents (Mythos Preview Alignment Risk Update, prior "alignment risk updates" in unit 59). So the lineage includes *alignment risk updates* as a genre beside Risk Reports. Correcting my unit-26 note: "prior risk reports" plural may reasonably include those.
