## Wandering

Reason 2 is notable: a threat is prioritised partly *as practice*. That's a methodological claim about the threat-selection rule that the project should record — threat models in this RSP are chosen not only by expected harm but by their value as a training ground. §6.1 "threat model criteria" may formalise it.

The capability trigger here ("roughly in line with … human research scientists at Anthropic") echoes the AI R&D threshold criterion (1) (substitute for the entire set of RS/RE). The two autonomy threat models share an anchor: Anthropic's own research staff. So Anthropic's workforce is the yardstick for "dangerous autonomy" in both.

Severity scope repeated: existential/global destabilisation. So, for misalignment, the relevant harm is existential-tier via future AI. In CB, "catastrophic" has the COVID-comparable anchor. These are different magnitudes under one word; I'll keep a running list in outline.md.
