# Unit 174 — §4.5 Risk mitigations: overview and Table 4.5.A, L2279–2317, pp.130–131

## What differed

- Safeguards vary on three axes: **robustness** (difficulty of jailbreaking; "Level 1/2/3"; improvements sometimes applied only to newer models), **coverage** (Fable 5 uses a new classifier, same robustness as other Mythos-class, "expanded domain of coverage, encompassing the vast majority of potentially dual use usage relevant to research biology"), **exemptions** (programmes for commercial users).
- **"Our previous risk report used 'ASL-3' and 'ASL-2' terminology to refer to both capability levels and safeguards, but our models and safeguards now vary along many dimensions, so we are no longer using this terminology."** — the ASL vocabulary is retired.
- "Classifier" = the end-to-end flag/block system, two stages (first-stage probe/classifier, second-stage classifier).
- **Table 4.5.A** (as converted; some robustness cells empty): Mythos 5 — Level 3, no exemptions programme; Fable 5 — Level 3 (Mythos 5 with additional safeguards, wider audience), none; Mythos Preview — legacy, not widely deployed, none; Opus 4.8 — "most capable model without higher-coverage bio classifiers offered widely to consumers", Bioclassifier Exemption Policy; Opus 4.7 — legacy, exemption policy; **Sonnet 5** — major commercial model ≈ Opus 4.8 in CB capability, Level 3, exemption policy; Opus 4.6, 4.5, Sonnet 4.6, 4.5 — Level 2 (some surfaces Level 1), exemption policy; Opus 4.1, Opus 4 — Level 1, exemption policy; **Sonnet 4 — "most capable model in risk-relevant domains not subject to real-time classifiers"**; Haiku 4.5 and older legacy — no blocking classifiers.

## Wandering

**ASL retired.** My unit-7 prediction ("RSP 3.x may have retired or demoted ASL levels") is confirmed by the report. For the project this is a major vocabulary fact: Anthropic's own documents before RSP 3.0 (and the Feb 2026 report) use "ASL-3/ASL-2" for both capability and safeguards; the August report replaces that conflated term with separate axes — thresholds (CB-1, CB-2, AI R&D criteria) and safeguards (robustness level, coverage level, exemptions). A translator mapping "ASL-3" across Anthropic documents must know it named two things at once and that the August report deliberately split them. Excellent lexicon material: a source correcting its own earlier term's ambiguity.

The model roster grows: Sonnet 5 exists (≈ Opus 4.8 for CB). And the lowest-safeguard frontier-ish model is Sonnet 4 — no real-time classifiers; its risk argued via limited capability (unit 166).
