# Unit 202 — §4.5.8.1 Relaxed weight-storage criteria, L2592–2600, pp.146–147

Snapshots not yet evaluated are assigned to secure compartments by a general-capability proxy (training loss) compared with evaluated models; the loss threshold was made "somewhat less conservative" since the previous report; some *activations* of more capable models may be stored outside the compartments in certain cases. Judged not a meaningful change in risk posture: the old threshold was highly conservative, and the new one still corresponds to models not easily convertible to Opus-4-level risk-relevant capability "even given nontrivial amounts of additional fine-tuning".

Differed: a disclosed deliberate *relaxation* of a security control, with reasons. Exactly what §4.5.8's intro promised.

Wandering: training loss as a proxy for CB risk is a capability-to-hazard bridge the project could record: the source uses a general capability metric to classify un-evaluated artefacts for security. Activations stored outside compartments — activations can leak information about a model (and could support distillation or reconstruction in principle); the report asserts no meaningful risk change without detail. Claim with thin public evidence; mark.
