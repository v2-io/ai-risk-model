# Unit 183 — BPJ mitigating points (1), L2392–2400, p.136

(Status-only.) Mitigating point 1: hard to implement efficiently — Anthropic's reimplementation with UK AISI's help took weeks (~20 hours of full-time effort, with AISI back-and-forth); attackers would lack redacted details and AISI support → "risks are low for our CB-1 threat model". Getting an efficient implementation is a "verifiable hill-climbable task" models are improving at; current models (Opus 4.5/4.6, Mythos Preview) weren't much help; expected to change for sufficiently capable models; monitored.

Differed: a nice self-referential risk: the jailbreak's main barrier (implementation difficulty) is the kind of barrier AI capability erodes. The report names this.

Wandering: here capability growth attacks a safeguard *indirectly* — better models help adversaries build attacks on the safeguards around models. Another instance of the report's recurring structure: present safety margins that are capability-limited and expected to shrink. For the project: a causal edge from model capability (coding/optimisation) to safeguard-bypass feasibility.
