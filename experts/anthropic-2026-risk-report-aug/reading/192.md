# Unit 192 — §4.5.5.1 Exemptions for Mythos-class models, L2467–2479, p.140

As of the coverage date, **no external commercial access to Mythos Preview, Mythos 5, or Fable 5 without blocking biology classifiers**; organisations exempt on lesser models are still classified on these. A future "trusted access program" with reduced/no bio safeguards possible. Unsafeguarded access briefly offered to a very limited pool of external testing organisations during predeployment testing; not since release. Two redactions (one continuing the BPJ-monitor redaction).

Differed: Glasswing customers get Mythos 5 *with* blocking bio classifiers (Level 3, standard coverage); Fable 5 general users get Level 3 *with wider coverage*. So the two regimes differ in coverage breadth, not in whether bio safeguards exist.

Wandering: the predeployment unsafeguarded access is the route by which external evaluators test Mythos-class models — consistent with the AISI cyber incident's "safeguards removed" setup (unit 34), though that was a cyber evaluation. An expert asked how external evaluators test these models should keep "unsafeguarded predeployment access to a very limited pool of testers" in view.
