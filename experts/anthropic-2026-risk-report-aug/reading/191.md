# Unit 191 — §4.5.5 Bioclassifier exemptions (intro), L2463–2465, p.140

Trusted, vetted users can access models with reduced or no CB blocking classifiers; where data retention agreements permit, classifier scores are still logged (no blocking). fn 67.

Differed: as expected from unit 190.

Wandering: the exemption is the access-control answer to the dual-use limit (unit 177): legitimate biology researchers need the capability; identity vetting substitutes for content blocking. Its strength is the vetting process and the post-hoc monitoring — both detailed next. Note that Mythos 5 / Fable 5 had "None as of this writing" for exemptions programmes (Table 4.5.A) — the most capable models have no exemption route; Glasswing access for Mythos 5 must be governed differently.
