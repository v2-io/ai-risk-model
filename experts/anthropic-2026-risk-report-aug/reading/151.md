# Unit 151 — §3.7.1 Mitigations planned for the AI R&D threshold, and progress, L1937–1957, pp.110–111

## What differed

Answers my unit-150 question: the **RSP's planned mitigations "by the time models reach this threshold"** (quoted from the RSP, in italics):
1. **"moonshot R&D for security"** — ambitious, possibly unconventional security against "the world's best-resourced attackers".
2. **"eyes on everything"** — comprehensively gather, centralise, maintain logs for all critical AI-development activities; AI analysis for security threats, "concerning behavior by insiders (humans as well as AI systems themselves)", and training processes/data "out of line with the public Constitution".
3. **Systematic alignment assessments** with mechanistic interpretability and adversarial red-teaming of auditing methods.
4. **Internal red-teaming of deployment safeguards better than the bug bounty participants collectively.**

Status (threshold not crossed, so not yet required):
- Moonshots: two laid out in the Frontier Safety Roadmap, not completed.
- Monitoring improved, but more powerful agents with more internal affordances enlarged the surface; **"we don't believe we meet an 'eyes on everything' standard as of the coverage date"**; target **January 1, 2027**.
- Alignment assessments: extensive, with NLAs and steered evaluations.
- **One** experiment (auditing methods as of Jan 2026 caught an "overt saboteur" organism, with human help) — "not a novel development since our previous Risk Report".
- Bio-classifier red-teaming "not yet systematic or very well resourced"; one notable jailbreak each from bug bounty and internal red-teaming this period; not strong evidence of meeting the bar.

## Wandering

This is the most important governance passage in §3: the RSP lists what must be in place *when* the threshold is reached, and the report shows most of it isn't in place now, with "eyes on everything" targeted for Jan 1, 2027. Combined with §3.5's "meaningful acceleration … <2×", lagged measurement, and Model 2 at 62.8% vs an 85% CoBench bar: the race is visible in the report's own numbers. If the threshold were crossed before the mitigations are ready, the RSP's requirements would bind on a company not yet meeting them. The report doesn't say what happens then (pause? deploy with gaps?). An expert asked "is Anthropic ready for its own AI R&D threshold?" should answer from this page: *by its own account, not yet; target dates exist.*

"Insiders (humans as well as AI systems themselves)" — AI systems explicitly classed as potential insider threats in RSP language. That's the misalignment threat model's premise written into a security requirement.

"Training processes or data that are out of line with the public Constitution" — the Constitution again as the alignment standard, and "public" emphasised: the published Constitution as the yardstick for what training should instil. For the project: the RSP binds training practice to a public normative document.

The "overt saboteur" experiment is counted honestly as old news (not since the last report). The report resists padding its progress list.
