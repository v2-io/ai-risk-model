# Unit 190 — §4.5.4 Offline monitoring (CB), L2451–2461, pp.139–140

Offline monitoring de-emphasised as untargeted jailbreak discovery (as in the previous report), but used by Enforcement for account-level behaviour: a BPJ-pattern monitor (fn 66); **weekly automated monitoring of flagged-but-not-blocked traffic from exemption holders (non-ZDR)**, reviewed by a limited pool of employees; routine account-level monitoring on a subset of traffic across CB harm areas and multiple (not all) surfaces — semantic search + online flags → automated rubric triage → severity-ranked list for limited reviewers.

Differed: exemption holders are *flagged but not blocked* — their dangerous-topic traffic passes, and is reviewed weekly after the fact. That's the control replacing blocking for vetted users.

Wandering: the shape mirrors the misalignment monitoring stack (automated triage → small human pool), with the same bandwidth bottleneck. And ZDR again excludes exemption holders' traffic from monitoring when enabled. For exemption holders on ZDR, neither blocking nor monitoring applies — only vetting. An expert asked "what checks exist on an exempt organisation with ZDR?" should say: vetting (manual), per this unit's logic; §4.5.5 may add.
